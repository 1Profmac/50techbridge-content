## WHAT TO STOP DOING

1. **Stop chasing pitch events.** They're designed to make judges feel powerful, not to fund you.
2. **Stop writing RFPs for contracts you can't win.** The system is rigged toward incumbents.
3. **Stop perfecting before publishing.** Done > perfect. Every time.
4. **Stop building tools when you should be creating content.** The video system is built. Use it.
5. **Stop comparing yourself to funded startups.** They have money and no mission. You have a mission and no money. Your advantage is authenticity.

## WHAT TO START DOING

1. **Post every Tuesday and Wednesday.** No exceptions. Set a phone alarm.
2. **Upload to YouTube every Thursday.** One video. Already rendered. Just upload.
3. **Comment on 10 LinkedIn posts every Friday.** In your space — workforce, agetech, HR, L&D.
4. **Run the board meeting every Monday.** Accountability is the only thing between you and 10K.
5. **Pitch 5 speaking opportunities per month.** Conferences, workforce boards, community colleges, Rotary clubs, church groups. Start small, build the reel.
