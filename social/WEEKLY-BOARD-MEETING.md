## HOW TO RUN THE MEETING

Every Monday morning:
1. Open this file
2. Tell Claude: **"board meeting"**
3. Claude will pull your LinkedIn stats, website data, YouTube numbers
4. Walk through the agenda together
5. Set your 3 priorities for the week
6. Get back to work

### Rules
- 30 minutes max. No rambling.
- Numbers first, feelings second.
- Three priorities, not ten.
- If you skipped last week's priorities, say why — no judgment, just accountability.
- End every meeting with: "Does this get me closer to the stage?"
