## HeyGen-ready script
### (paste this directly into HeyGen — pause markers and emphasis are below)

People keep asking me about the numbers.

850 billion lost every year to age discrimination. 19 percent higher innovation revenue. Trained 60-year-olds outperforming untrained 25-year-olds. United Health. Cisco. BMW. CVS.

The numbers are real. But the data was never the point.

I built Learn More Technologies because I got tired of watching the most experienced people in every room get talked about like a problem to be managed.

The data is the door. It is not the room.

Behind the door is intention. The intention to stop apologizing for being experienced.

Behind the door is action. 347 Pioneers. Three times the industry completion rate.

Behind the door is invitation. A safe place to learn — built specifically for adults 50 and over.

Behind the door is community. Bidirectional mentorship. Buddies. People.

Behind the door is independence. Devices that stop being ornaments and start being assistants.

And behind the door is security. Because dignity is the foundation, not the afterthought.

So when someone tells you the 50+ workforce is a problem to manage — remember the numbers, then remember the room.

The data is the door.

The room is open.

### Tone direction for the avatar

- **Slower than the data episodes.** Parts 1–3 are punchy. This is contemplative.
- **First sentence is conversational** ("People keep asking me about the numbers").
- **The numbers list is matter-of-fact, not enthusiastic.** Brian is reciting them like he's tired of repeating them — that's the point.
- **"But the data was never the point"** is the pivot. Slight emphasis on *was*.
- **The 6 "Behind the door is..." lines are the rhythmic spine.** Same intonation pattern each time. Liturgical.
- **The closing two lines ("The data is the door. The room is open.") should land with weight.** Slow. Confident. Final.

## Production workflow

1. Paste the **HeyGen-ready script** (top section, no brackets) into HeyGen
2. Render against navy `#0E1C2F` background (no chromakey)
3. Export as MP4
4. Build the Canva b-roll separately using `bridge-video-canva-broll-script.md`
5. Layer Brian's HeyGen render over the Canva b-roll in your video editor:
   - Full-frame on Segments 01, 03, 04, 12
   - PIP bottom-right on Segments 02, 05, 06–11
   - Fade out completely on Segments 13–14
6. Add soft outro music over Segments 13–14 only
7. Export as MP4 1920×1080
8. Create LANDSCAPE / SQUARE / VERTICAL variants for cross-platform posting
