# Bridge Video — Canva B-Roll Script
## "$850 Billion Series — Bridge Episode: It's Not About the Data"

**Duration:** ~90 seconds (210-word HeyGen narration)
**Format:** 1920×1080 landscape, navy bg `#0E1C2F`, gold accents `#C8942E`
**Fonts:** Playfair Display (italic for emphasis) + DM Sans (Bold/Medium/Regular)
**Brian PIP:** HeyGen avatar bottom-right inset OR full-frame at key beats
**Tone:** SOFT. SLOW. HUMAN. This is the OPPOSITE of the stats videos — minimal text, lots of breathing room, no 200pt numbers shouting at the viewer
**Brand rules:** No chromakey · No banned stats · Navy bg locked · Gold accent only on key words

---

## Master design rules for this video

1. **Most segments are mostly empty.** Negative space is the point. The viewer should *feel* the slowdown after the data-heavy episodes.
2. **No big numbers as the headline.** The numbers belong to the data episodes. This episode strips them out.
3. **One word or one phrase per slide.** Playfair Display Italic, large but not enormous (140–200px), centered, gold for the key word.
4. **Subtle motion only.** Slow fade in (0.8s), hold, slow fade out (0.8s). No swoops, no zooms, no flashy transitions.
5. **Brian's HeyGen avatar should be visible for ~70% of the runtime.** This is a face episode, not a graphics episode.

## Segment-by-segment script

- **Lower-third:** None
- **Note:** Open on Brian. No graphics. Let his face land first.

### Segment 02 — Numbers wash (00:06–00:18) · 12 sec
- **Visual:** Brian inset bottom-right (320×180px). Center-screen text that fades in line by line, each on its own beat:
  - Line 1: `$850 BILLION`
  - Line 2: `19% INNOVATION LIFT`
  - Line 3: `60-YEAR-OLDS OUTPERFORMING 25-YEAR-OLDS`
  - Line 4: `UNITED HEALTH · CISCO · BMW · CVS`
- **Font:** DM Sans Bold, 56px, white `#FFFFFF`, letter-spacing 0.08em
- **Effect:** Each line fades in over 0.5s, holds 2s, then ALL four lines DESATURATE to muted gray `#3a4555` simultaneously at the end of segment
- **Narration:** *"850 billion lost every year. 19 percent higher innovation revenue. Trained 60-year-olds outperforming untrained 25-year-olds. United Health. Cisco. BMW. CVS."*
- **Note:** This is the only segment that shows numbers. They appear, then drain of color — visualizing "the data was never the point."

## Why this works as a "bridge episode"

- **Stats are present, then drained.** Segment 02 shows the data, then desaturates it. Visually proves the message: "the data was never the point."
- **Six themes mirror the article.** Segments 06–11 walk through INTENTION → ACTION → INVITATION → COMMUNITY → INDEPENDENCE → SECURITY in the same order as the article. People who watched this video can read the article and feel the through-line. People who read the article first will watch this and recognize it.
- **Brian on screen 70% of the time.** This isn't a graphics video. It's a *founder talking* video. The graphics support him; they don't replace him.
- **Zero "Watch Part 4" cliffhanger.** This isn't a teaser for Part 4. It's a standalone manifesto that lives between Parts 3 and 4. People will share it because it's not asking them for anything.
