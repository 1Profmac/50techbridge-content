## Quick reference — the 6 motion techniques

| # | Name | Canva action | When to use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1** | Slow Zoom (Ken Burns) | Animate → Pan | Every b-roll clip, default |
| **T2** | Slow Pan (LR/RL) | Oversize video + Animate Pan | Wide shots, reveals |
| **T3** | Punch-In on Stats | 2 pages, 100%→110%, Dissolve + Bounce In | Stat reveals |
| **T4** | Scene Change Cuts | Page transitions: Dissolve / Hard / Fade-Black / Slide | Between segments |
| **T5** | L-Cut | Separate voice MP3, mute on-cam pages | Smooth audio over visual cuts |
| **T6** | The Hold | No animation, 5+ sec static | ONE per video, key moment |

## SEGMENT 02 — Numbers Wash (12 sec) ⚡ TWO PAGES

This segment is the only one that needs SPLITTING into two Canva pages to handle the desaturate effect.

### Page 2A — Stats appear in white (8 sec)

| Layer | Motion | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Brian PIP bottom-right** | None | He's just an inset, holding |
| **Stat lines (4)** | T3 — staggered Bounce In | Each line bounces in 0:00, 0:02, 0:04, 0:06 |
| **Page transition** | T4 — None (hard cut to 2B) | Match cut, no dissolve |

### Page 2B — Stats drained to gray (4 sec)

| Layer | Motion | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Brian PIP bottom-right** | None | Same position |
| **Same 4 stat lines** | Color: drained gray `#3a4555` | All on screen, no animation, just sit |
| **Page transition OUT** | T4 — Dissolve (0.5s) | Into the pivot |

**Why:** The hard cut from 2A → 2B visualizes the moment when "the data was never the point." The stats appear bright, then drain. T3 punch-in on the stats + T4 hard cut = "the data dies on screen."

## SEGMENT 13 — Closing Card (8 sec) ⭐ THE HOLD

This is the **T6 Hold** of the episode. The one moment of stillness.

| Layer | Motion | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Background** | Navy, NO video, NO Brian | He's gone. Just the words. |
| **"THE DATA IS THE DOOR."** | Fade In at 0:00 (0.8s) | DM Sans Bold 64px white |
| **"The room is open."** | Fade In at 0:02 (delayed 2s) | Playfair Italic 140px gold ← LARGER than the previous line, hierarchy |
| **HOLD** | No animation. Sit on this for the full 4 seconds before the transition. | THIS IS THE T6 HOLD. Don't move anything. Let it breathe. |
| **Page transition OUT** | T4 — Dissolve (1.0s, slower than usual) | Slow exit into the end card |

**Why this is the Hold:** The whole video has been moving — pans, zooms, cuts, fades. This single page has zero motion for 4 full seconds. After all that activity, the stillness HITS. The viewer's eye locks on "The room is open." That's the moment they remember.

**LMT rule:** You only get ONE Hold per video. This is it. Don't try to add another one anywhere else.

# MOTION TECHNIQUE USAGE SUMMARY

| Technique | Used in segments | Count |
|---|---|---|
| **T1 — Slow Zoom (Pan)** | 01, 03, 04, 05 (PIP), 06–11 (PIP), 12 | 12 segments |
| **T2 — Slow Pan (LR/RL)** | Not used in bridge — too cinematic for this manifesto piece | 0 |
| **T3 — Punch-In on Stats** | 02 (stat lines), 07 (Bounce In on 347/3X) | 2 segments |
| **T4 — Scene Change Cuts** | Every transition (Dissolve default, Fade-Black at 04→05 and 11→12, Hard Cut at 2A→2B) | All 13 transitions |
| **T5 — L-Cut** | Optional — apply if you export Brian's voice separately as MP3 | 0 (skip on first build) |
| **T6 — The Hold** | Segment 13 ONLY | 1 segment |

**Total Holds:** 1 ✅ (rule says exactly 1 per video)
**Total Fade-to-Blacks:** 2 ✅ (one at end of intro phase, one at end of manifesto body)
**Total Hard Cuts:** 1 ✅ (only between Page 2A and Page 2B for the desaturate effect)
**Default transition:** Dissolve (used 10 times)

# BUILD ORDER (do in this sequence to save time)

1. **Build Page 6 (INTENTION) first** — it's the master template for Pages 7–11
2. **Duplicate Page 6 → 5 times** — gives you Pages 7, 8, 9, 10, 11
3. **Edit only the text** on each duplicate (pill label + headline)
4. **Build Page 1, 3, 4, 12** — the Brian-on-camera pages (similar layouts)
5. **Build Page 2A and 2B** — the numbers wash (most complex)
6. **Build Page 5** — the metaphor (text-heavy)
7. **Build Page 13 — THE HOLD** — most important, build last when you're warmed up
8. **Build Page 14** — end card (template you can reuse from Part 3)
9. **Apply page transitions** in one pass at the end (don't interrupt page-building flow)
10. **Add audio** last
11. **Preview the full video** before exporting
12. **Export** master + variants
