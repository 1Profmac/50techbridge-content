# $850 Billion Series — Marketing Playbook

## Critical LinkedIn Rules

- **Native upload only** — never post a YouTube link (algorithm kills reach)
- **First 3 seconds = everything** — open mid-thought with a bold stat, not "Hi, I'm Brian"
- **Text overlay hook on frame 1** so it reads before play
- **3-5 hashtags max** (#WorkforceDevelopment #AgeTech #DigitalSkills #WIOA #AdultLearning)
- **Engage in comments within first hour** — algorithm rewards early engagement
- **Post 9-11 AM Tuesday/Thursday**

---

## The Comment-as-Hook Technique (Your "Auto-Selected Comments")

This is your most powerful move:

1. Post Part 1 → collect best comments over the week
2. **Part 2 opens with:** full-screen comment overlay (white text on navy card, 3-5 seconds) → then you respond to it directly on camera
3. Repeat for Parts 3 and 4

This simultaneously delivers: **social proof + community signal + instant hook + re-engagement of the original commenter** (who will reshare).

### Design Spec for Comment Overlays

- Navy card (#0E1C2F) with subtle gold (#C8942E) border
- White text (#FFFFFF), 48-60pt
- Commenter name + title below in smaller text
- 3-5 seconds on screen
- Max 3-4 overlays per video part

## /workforce Page Video Placement

### Above the Fold

- **60-90 second sizzle reel** (best moments from all 4 parts) — click-to-play with custom thumbnail
- CTA button ("Schedule a Free Consult") immediately adjacent to video
- Use **facade/lite loading** — show thumbnail first, load player on click (protects page speed)

### Below the Fold

- Full 4-part series as a tabbed or playlist interface
- Part 4 (conversion piece) gets prominent placement near the pricing/program options section
- Comment overlays displayed as static testimonial cards between sections

### Impact Data

- Landing pages with video convert up to **86% better**
- 65% of visitors will watch a video under 60 seconds
- Full 17 min should NOT be the hero — save it for YouTube

---

## YouTube Strategy

### Full-Length (17 min)

- Upload complete version with **chapter markers** (timestamps for each of the 4 arcs)
- 200+ word keyword-rich description
- Custom thumbnail: your face + bold text + high contrast
- Cards linking to each individual part

### Shorts (5-8 clips)

- YouTube Shorts algorithm is **completely separate** from long-form — it's a distinct discovery channel
- Extract your boldest 15-30 second moments
- Each Short ends with: "Full series linked in bio"
- Creators posting both formats grow subscribers **3x faster**

## The Authority Flywheel

### 1. Thought Leader Ads on LinkedIn

Boost your personal profile video posts (not the LMT company page). This format **dramatically outperforms** standard ads and is massively underutilized in B2B. Even $50-100/week on Part 1 can generate significant reach among your Tier 1 targets (Workforce Solutions Capital Area, Texas Workforce Commission, etc.).

### 2. Content-to-DM Pipeline

In your LinkedIn posts, offer a resource: "Comment FRAMEWORK to get the workforce training checklist." This generates 10-30 qualified leads per month without ad spend, and gives you permission to start conversations in DMs with workforce managers and HR leaders.

### 3. Transcript-to-Blog SEO Play

Transcribe each video part → publish as a blog post on learnmoretechnologies.com with embedded video + full transcript. Google indexes the text (video alone doesn't rank for long-tail searches). This captures organic traffic from people searching workforce development terms.

### 4. Serialized Identity Formation

By releasing weekly with consistent naming ("The Workforce Series, Part 3 of 4"), you trigger **identity-based engagement** — viewers start thinking of themselves as "someone who follows this series." This is psychologically stickier than one-off content and builds the committed audience your 50,000 Pioneer goal needs.

### 5. Stagger, Don't Batch

Don't post all derivative content on day one. Spread micro-content across 2-3 weeks per video part. This maintains constant presence without exhausting material — one 17-minute video feeds 8+ weeks of content.

### 6. The WIOA Connection

Your Tier 1 targets (Texas workforce boards) use LinkedIn to scout training providers. A professional 4-part series demonstrating your methodology + results (347 Pioneers, 3X completion) is essentially a **video capability statement**. Tag relevant organizations authentically in your posts.
