## Video 1: The Retention Reality
**Theme:** The hidden cost of high turnover in younger workers vs. the stability of the 50+ workforce.

### Script
"The investment gap isn't just an accident—it's a calculation error. We spend half as much training **adults 50+** as those under 35, assuming a longer return. But the data tells a different story. Workers under 30 quit at three times the rate of **Pioneers**. The 29-year-old you invested in walks out before the training pays off. The **Pioneer** you overlooked? They would have stayed a decade."

## Video 2: Appreciating Assets
**Theme:** Redefining **adults 50+** as assets that grow in value through institutional knowledge and mentorship.

They lose the mentorship infrastructure that keeps a workforce guided. And they lose alignment with the consumers who control 70% of disposable income. It’s time to see **Pioneers** as an appreciating asset."

## Video 4: The Math of Upskilling
**Theme:** The economic common sense of training existing employees over hiring new ones.

### Script
"The math is not close. Upskilling an existing employee costs six times less than hiring externally for the same capability. Replacing a worker can cost up to 200% of their annual salary, while training costs just over $1,200 a year. Investing in your **Pioneers** isn't just the right thing to do—it's the most profitable decision you can make."
