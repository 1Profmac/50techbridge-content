# 50+TechBridge Sizzle Reel B-Roll: The $850 Billion Workforce Decision

This Sizzle Reel B-roll is designed for the "$850 Billion Workforce Page," incorporating the LearnMoreTechnologies brand voice, terminology, and visual identity (Navy Blue and Vibrant Orange). It aims for a high-energy, impactful presentation, drawing inspiration from the previously generated B-roll graphics.

## Sizzle Reel Script

"The clock is ticking. Every year, age discrimination costs our economy $850 billion. That's the price of overlooking **Pioneers** – the experienced **adults 50+** who drive innovation and stability. They stay longer, perform better, and hold 70% of disposable income. Yet, we let institutional knowledge walk out the door, and defund programs that triple employment rates. The workforce cliff is here, and the cost of inaction is measured in trillions. Stop losing what you already have. Invest in your **Pioneers**. Secure your future. **50+TechBridge**: Ageism is the problem. AgeTech is the solution. And you are the bridge."
