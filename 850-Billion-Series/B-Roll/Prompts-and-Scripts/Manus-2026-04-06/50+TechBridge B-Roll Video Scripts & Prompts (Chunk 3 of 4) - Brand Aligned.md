**Theme:** Re-evaluating the return on investment for **Pioneers** and the tangible benefits of their experience.

A 56-year-old **Pioneer** has years of productivity ahead, and upskilling programs pay for themselves in just 12 to 18 months. Companies like BMW and CVS prove it: higher productivity, reduced defects, lower turnover, and increased customer satisfaction. Experience isn't a cost; it's a powerful investment."

## Video 4: The Urgency & Economic Impact
**Theme:** The critical urgency of the aging workforce crisis and its massive economic implications.

### Script
"The urgency is not going away. By 2030, one in five Americans will be over 65. The workforce cliff is here: millions of manufacturing jobs, physician shortages, and utility workers nearing retirement. This isn't a distant threat; it's an immediate crisis. Inaction costs trillions. Closing employment gaps for **Pioneers** could add $1.3 trillion to U.S. GDP annually. Age discrimination already costs $850 billion. This isn't just social; it's about revenue, market standing, and the future of our economy. Act now, or be left behind."
