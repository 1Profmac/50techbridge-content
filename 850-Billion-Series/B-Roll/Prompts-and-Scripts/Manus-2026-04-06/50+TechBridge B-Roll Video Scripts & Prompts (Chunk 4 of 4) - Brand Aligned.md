## Video 1: The Digital Divide & AI Opportunity
**Theme:** The critical need for digital skills among **adults 50+** and their rapid adoption of AI.

### Script
"92 percent of jobs demand digital skills, yet 22 million **Pioneers** lack broadband. One-third of U.S. workers are digitally underserved, with **adults 50+** facing the largest gap. But here's the truth: AI adoption among **Pioneers** has tripled in just two years, from 9 to 30 percent! The challenge isn't demand; it's access. We're bridging that gap, empowering **Pioneers** to master AI and digital platforms, transforming overlooked talent into competitive assets."

## Video 2: The Proof & The Paradox
**Theme:** Digital literacy initiatives triple employment, yet face defunding – highlighting the paradox of proven investment.

### Script
"Data proves it: digital literacy initiatives work. RAND analyzed a program that nearly tripled employment rates for **Pioneers**, from 14 to 40 percent, in just two months. One digital skill boosts earnings by 23 percent; three or more, by 45 percent! Yet, this program, which delivered undeniable results, was defunded. The pattern is clear: pilot it, prove it, then defund it. We refuse to let that happen."
