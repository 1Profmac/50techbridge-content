About Brian

I'm still in the arena.
Not the bleachers.

I built my company with a folding table and a public library. No venture capital. No team of engineers. Just three decades of experience, an unshakable belief that adults 50+ deserve better — and the willingness to prove it.

Today, Learn More Technologies runs on AI agents. Eight agent roles. One human CEO. It's not a concept or a pitch — it's how I operate every single day. When I stand in front of your workforce or your conference room, I'm not selling theory. I'm showing you what I've already built.

I spent 15 years in community development before founding LMT. I've trained adults in public libraries, workforce centers, senior centers, and corporate training rooms across Central Texas. I know what it feels like to be told your best years are behind you — and I know what it takes to prove that wrong.

The $76 trillion opportunity. That's how much wealth adults 50+ control in America — 52% of all U.S. net worth. Most organizations are ignoring it while simultaneously pushing out the workforce that generates it. I'm here to fix that — one organization, one workforce, one Pioneer at a time.
