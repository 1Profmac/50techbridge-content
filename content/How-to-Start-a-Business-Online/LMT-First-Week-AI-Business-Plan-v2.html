The First Week AI Business Plan

For Adults 50+ · The tool stack + mindset + 7-day plan
By Brian McKinney · Founder, Learn More Technologies
The Premise

The same AI tools being used to downsize experienced workers are the exact tools that let one experienced person build and run an entire business assistant for under $120 a month. You don't need a team. You need the stack, the mindset, and seven days.

The Tool Stack
ToolCostPurpose
Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus $20/mo Your AI business partner — strategy, writing, research
Canva Pro $14/mo Visuals, decks, social graphics
Google Workspace $7.20/mo Email, docs, calendar, storage
Calendly $12/mo Booking and scheduling
Domain + hosting $15/mo Your online home
Buffer $6/mo per channel Social scheduling without the grind
Stripe Free + 2.9% + 30¢/txn Get paid
Notion or Airtable Free tier Run your operation

Total: roughly $70–$120/month depending on your stack and billing cycle.

Tip: Annual billing saves 15–20% on most tools. Canva drops to ~$10/mo, Calendly to $10/mo.

The Mindset
  • Your experience is not a hurdle. It is your launchpad.
  • You don't need permission. Over half of U.S. businesses are owned by people 55+.
  • AI is not replacing you. It is force-multiplying you.
The 7-Day Plan
DayAction
1Pick the problem you know how to solve. Write it in one sentence.
2Set up your tool stack. Claude or ChatGPT, Canva, Calendly, Google.
3Build your offer page. One page. One offer. One price.
4Draft your first 3 LinkedIn posts. Use AI to draft. Your voice to finish.
5Post. Message 10 people who might need what you offer.
6Take your first call. Free if needed. Proof over price this week.
7Deliver. Ask for a testimonial. Repeat next week.
