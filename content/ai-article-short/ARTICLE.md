# AI Is Already In Your Life

Have you ever felt overwhelmed by how quickly technology changes? Whether you're texting a friend, streaming a movie, or managing your finances online, AI is now woven into daily life, often in ways you don't notice. For adults 50+, this can feel exciting and overwhelming. But you're not alone, and you're more capable than you realize.

Adapting to these changes doesn't have to be intimidating. With the right support and mindset, you can thrive in a world shaped by new technology.

## Change Happens Fast

I've witnessed just how quickly technology can upend daily life. Not long ago, government and medical services required face-to-face visits or mailed paperwork, and music came from CDs. A trip to Blockbuster was a Friday night ritual. Today, nearly everything is done online — often with little time to adjust.
