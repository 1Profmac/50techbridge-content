# LinkedIn Post — April 22, 2026
## "Influence Is Not Who You Know"

---

I think we got influence wrong.

We measure it by the size of our network.
By the amount of capital we control.
By the number of people who know our name.

I disagree.

Influence is not the number of people you connect with.
It's the number of people you help.

Not pitch. Not sell to. Not "add value" to.
Help. Actually help. Did their life get better because you showed up?

Here's what I know about the people we serve:

Babies are just little people. Mothers and fathers are the ones who provide, who hold the family together, who care for the children. But grandparents — grandparents are the ones who built the structure. They support the parents when the bottom falls out. When the ground gets soft. They built it. They paid for it. And they are still contributing to it.

And the world is telling them they're done.

I left a career inside one of the largest advocacy organizations in America because I watched billions of dollars get stuck in bureaucratic traffic jams while the people that money was supposed to reach fell further behind every day.

Nobody was coming to help the people who hold families together. So I built it myself.

Learn More Technologies trains adults 50+ in AI and digital skills. Not because it's a good market. Because the people who built the structure deserve the tools to keep contributing to it. Teaching them AI isn't charity. It's respect.

Our program runs at 3X the industry completion rate. Not because we have better technology. Because we show up. Boots on the ground. Online and offline. Senior centers, libraries, churches, kitchen tables, Zoom rooms. Wherever the people are.

Each one teach one. That's the model. That's how 50,000 Pioneers happens. Not through funding. Through people helping people.

Funders are looking for returns.
We're looking for the 62-year-old who was afraid to open a laptop and is now running her business with AI.
The 58-year-old veteran who thought the workforce passed him by and just built his first digital portfolio.
The grandmother who FaceTimed her grandkids for the first time and cried.

That's influence.
Everything else is just networking.
