## "AI Is for Young Tech People"
### By Brian McKinney · Founder, Learn More Technologies · #AGENTIC50

**The Myth**

Walk into any AI conference and count the gray hair. You'll run out of fingers fast.

The marketing says it all — hoodies, hackathons, "disruptors" in their twenties. The message is clear: AI is a young person's game. If you're over 50, you missed the boat.

That message is wrong. And it's expensive.

**The Reality**

AI doesn't need coding skills. It needs context.

When you prompt Claude or ChatGPT, you're not writing software. You're giving instructions to a very fast, very literal colleague who knows everything and understands nothing. The quality of the output depends entirely on the quality of the input.

And who gives better instructions — a 24-year-old with two years of work experience, or a 55-year-old who spent three decades solving the exact problem they're describing?

This isn't a guess. We tested it.

At Learn More Technologies, 347 adults over 50 have completed our AI training program. Our completion rate is 3X the industry average. 74% report increased confidence using AI professionally.

The reason is simple: experienced professionals don't need to learn what problems to solve. They already know. They just need the tool.

**The Application — Try This Today**

Open Claude (claude.ai) or ChatGPT. No account needed for a basic prompt. Type this:

> "I'm a [your profession] with [X] years of experience. My biggest daily frustration is [specific task]. Give me 3 ways AI could handle that task, with step-by-step instructions for each."

That's it. That's your first AI session.

What you'll notice: the AI doesn't give you a generic answer. It gives you a specific one — because YOU gave it specific context. Your experience isn't a barrier to using AI. It's the reason AI works better for you than for someone with no experience at all.
