What Smart Organizations Do Differently

  • Build upskilling pathways designed for experienced adults — not watered-down programs built for digital natives
  • Implement bidirectional mentorship — experienced workers teach institutional knowledge; newer colleagues teach platform fluency
  • Rethink the ROI timeline — measure actual retention, productivity impact, and knowledge transfer outcomes
  • Treat experienced workers as an asset class that deserves the same investment as every other talent segment
