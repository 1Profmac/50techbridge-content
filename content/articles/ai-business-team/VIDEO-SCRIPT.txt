VIDEO SCRIPT — "You Already Have a Business. You Just Don't Know It Yet."
Source: Book Chapter 10b — Your AI Business Team
Format: YouTube / LinkedIn Video | Target length: 8–10 minutes

[HOOK — first 15 seconds, no title card yet]

"Three months ago, a woman named Margaret came up to me after one of my free
tech talks at a senior center in Austin.

She said five words that I haven't been able to stop thinking about.

She said: 'Is there a way to get paid for this?'

Stay with me. Because her answer changed her life —
and it might change yours."

---

[TITLE CARD: You Already Have a Business. You Just Don't Know It Yet.]
[Lower third: Brian McKinney | 50+TechBridge | learnmoretechnologies.com]

---

[SEGMENT 1 — THE STORY | ~90 seconds]

"Margaret is 61 years old. She spent six years as the full-time caregiver
for her mother — managing medications, coordinating multiple doctors,
translating medical language for the family, handling insurance paperwork.

When her mother passed, Margaret came to one of our Lunch and Learn sessions.
And after the session, she found me and asked that question.

'I've been doing this my whole life — taking care of people, managing
complicated situations, solving problems nobody else wanted to touch.
Is there a way to actually get paid for this?'

I looked at her and said: yes. The answer is yes.
And the reason the answer is yes now, more than ever before in history,
is AI."

---

[SEGMENT 2 — THE REFRAME | ~60 seconds]

"Here's what most people over 50 don't realize:

You don't need to learn technology to use AI for your business.

You need to know what question to ask.

You have spent fifty-plus years learning to ask the right questions —
in the operating room, on the construction site, in the classroom,
in the boardroom, in the kitchen, at the bedside.

That's the skill. AI is just the new tool.

You don't need to understand how it works.
You need to understand what you know —
and trust that the world will pay for it.

So let me show you exactly how. Seven steps.
All free. Starting today."

---

[SEGMENT 3 — STEP 1 | ~45 seconds]
[GRAPHIC: "Step 1: Name What You Know"]

"Before you open any app, grab a piece of paper.
Write down five things you've done in your life that other people
found hard or confusing.

Not things you were paid for.
Things you just DID — because you knew how.
Because someone needed it done.
Because you were the person in the room who understood it.

That list? That's your business.
Everything else we're going to talk about today
is just the infrastructure to deliver it."

---

[SEGMENT 4 — STEP 2 | ~60 seconds]
[GRAPHIC: "Step 2: Build Your Business Plan in 30 Minutes"]
[SCREEN SHARE or B-ROLL: ChatGPT / Claude interface]

"Open ChatGPT — free at chat.openai.com — or Claude at claude.ai.
Both are free. Both work on your phone.

Type this exact sentence:

'I want to start a small business helping [what you do].
My customers are [who needs this].
Help me build a simple one-page business plan.'

That's it.

In five minutes you will have a business plan
that would have cost a consultant two weeks and several thousand dollars.

It gives you your service name, your pricing, your target customer,
your first five marketing ideas.

Print it. Put it on the wall.
You are now a business owner."

---

[SEGMENT 5 — STEPS 3 & 4 | ~60 seconds]
[GRAPHIC: "Step 3: Name Your Business | Step 4: Get Online — Free"]

"Same AI tool, next prompt:

'Help me come up with five names for my business
and write a one-sentence description of what I offer.'

Then ask it to write your professional bio.

For your website — go to Wix.com or Squarespace.com.
Both have free plans. Both have templates you fill in like a form.
Ask the AI to write every word.

For Google — go to business.google.com and create a free profile.
This puts you on Google Maps and Google Search.

Website and Google presence.
Cost: zero dollars.
Time: one afternoon."

---

[SEGMENT 6 — STEP 5 | ~45 seconds]
[GRAPHIC: "Step 5: Find Your First Client"]

"This is the step most people skip, and it's the most important one.

The fastest path to your first paying client is not an ad.
It's a list of ten people who already know you.

Ask the AI:
'Help me write a short message to send to friends and family
introducing my new business. Personal, not salesy.'

Send it to ten people.

One of those ten knows someone who needs exactly what you do.

That's your first client. I promise you."

---

[SEGMENT 7 — STEP 6 | ~45 seconds]
[GRAPHIC: "Step 6: The Free Business Stack"]

"Once you have clients, here's how you stay organized —
all free, all on your phone or computer:

Google Calendar — your schedule.
Gmail — your professional email.
Google Docs — your proposals, contracts, notes.
Wave.com — free invoicing and accounting.
Canva — business cards, flyers, social graphics.
ChatGPT or Claude — your writer and strategist.

Monthly cost: zero dollars.

That's a complete business infrastructure.
For free."

---

[SEGMENT 8 — STEP 7 | ~45 seconds]
[GRAPHIC: "Step 7: The Daily Habit"]

"Every morning, before you start work, open your AI tool and ask one question.

'I have a client meeting today. What should I ask to understand their needs?'

Or: 'Help me write a follow-up email that's professional and not pushy.'

Or: 'How should I price this project? Here's what's involved.'

The AI gives you a starting point. A first draft.
Something to react to rather than create from scratch.

One conversation. Every morning.
That's the habit that runs the business."

---

[SEGMENT 9 — MARGARET'S OUTCOME | ~45 seconds]

"Here's what happened with Margaret.

Three months after that coffee conversation,
she launched Care Navigator Consulting.

She helps families of adults with complex medical needs
do exactly what she spent six years doing for her mother.

She charges $75 an hour. She has four clients.
She works twenty hours a week. From home.

She is not a tech person.

She's a person who spent fifty years becoming expert at something
the world desperately needs —
and who finally found the tools to turn that expertise into income.

'I did it after 50,' she told me last month.
'I kept hearing you say that. I decided to believe you.'"

Your relationships build the business.

Open one conversation.
Type one question.
Take one step.

I did it after 50. And so did Margaret.

So can you."
