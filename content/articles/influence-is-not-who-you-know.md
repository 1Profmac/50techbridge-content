# Influence Is Not Who You Know

**By Professor Brian McKinney | Founder, Learn More Technologies**

---

I think we got influence wrong.

Somewhere along the way, we started measuring it by the size of our network. By the amount of capital we control. By the number of people who know our name. The bigger the Rolodex, the more powerful you are. The more zeros on the check, the more influence you carry.

I spent years inside that system. I watched it from the inside. And I'm here to tell you that the definition of influence is broken.

**Influence is not the number of people you connect with. It's the number of people you help.**

Not pitch. Not sell to. Not "add value" to. Help. Actually, tangibly, it helps. Did their life get better because you showed up? Did they learn something they couldn't learn before? Did they gain confidence they didn't have yesterday?

If the answer is no, you don't have influence. You have a contact list.

---

## The People Who Built the Structure

Let me tell you who I fight for.

Babies are just little people. They come into this world needing everything. Mothers and fathers are the ones who provide, who hold the family together, who sacrifice, who show up every morning whether they feel like it or not. They carry the weight.

But grandparents are the ones who built the foundation underneath it all.

They're the ones the parents call when the bottom falls out. When the money runs short. When the marriage cracks. When the ground gets soft, and nothing feels stable anymore. Grandparents don't just support the family; they are the foundation the family stands on. They built it. They paid for it. They sacrificed for it. And most of them are still contributing to it right now.

And the world is telling them they're done.

Too old to learn. Too slow to keep up. Too expensive to retrain. Too far behind to catch up. That's what the market says. That's what the job postings imply. That's what the AI headlines scream every single day: technology is moving forward, and experienced workers are being left behind.

THAT'S A LIE.

---

## Nobody Was Coming

I worked within one of America's largest advocacy organizations serving adults 50 and older. I was committed to the mission and genuinely believed the system would deliver on its promise.

Here's what I learned: billions of dollars are allocated every year for workforce development, digital inclusion, and training programs for older adults. Billions.

And most of that money gets stuck in bureaucratic traffic jams, committee reviews, application cycles, compliance paperwork, and pilot programs that take 18 months to launch and serve only a select few.

Meanwhile, the people that money is supposed to reach are falling further behind every single day.

I waited for someone to fix it. I waited for the big institutions to move faster. I waited for the programs to reach the communities. I waited for the system to catch up to the urgency.

Nobody came.

That's why we're building 50+TechBridge ourselves.

---

## Boots on the Ground

Learn More Technologies exists because I stopped waiting for permission.

We train adults 50 and older in AI and digital skills. Not because it's a good market opportunity. Not because some investor saw a trend line. Because 22 million adults in this country are digitally underserved, and the most experienced workers in America are being told they can't learn the tools that are reshaping every industry on earth.

That's not a market gap. That's an injustice.

Our program runs at 3X the industry completion rate. And I'll tell you why — it's not better technology. It's not a fancier platform. It's not some proprietary algorithm.

It's because we show up.

Boots on the ground. Online and offline. Senior centers. Libraries. Churches. Community centers. Employer break rooms. Kitchen tables. Zoom rooms at 7 PM because that's when the grandkids are asleep, and grandma finally has 45 minutes to learn something new.

We go where the people are. We don't wait for them to find us. We don't hide behind an application portal. We don't require three forms of ID and a committee vote before someone can learn how to use AI to write a better resume.

We just teach.

---

## Each One Teach One

Here's the part that the funders don't always understand.

50+TechBridge is not a product. It's not a course you buy. It's a movement you join.

Our goal is 50,000 Pioneers. Not customers, Pioneers. People who learn, then teach. Each one teach one. That's the model. That's how it scales. Not through venture capital. Not through institutional approval. Through people helping people.

One person learns AI. They show their neighbor. Their coworker. Their church group. Their VFW post. Their senior center. The knowledge spreads the way knowledge is supposed to spread — person to person, community to community, generation to generation.

The foundational lessons are free. Always have been. Always will be. Because the goal was never to build a paywall. The goal was to build a bridge.

This is too big for one person. Too big for one company. Too big for one grant. Too big for one city. That's the point. 50+TechBridge is built to be replicated, not controlled. Plug-and-play. Any community. Any partner. Any facilitator who cares enough to show up.

We don't scale through sales. We scale through service.

## What Funders Want vs. What We Want

Funders are looking for returns. Investors want metrics. Boards want numbers that justify their budgets and impress their stakeholders.

I understand that world. I respect it. We track our data. We measure our outcomes. We can deliver a 3X completion rate, a 74% increase in confidence, and a 30-day deployment timeline. The numbers are real, and they're audit-ready.

But that's not why I do this.

We do this for the 62-year-old woman who was afraid to open a laptop three months ago and is now running her small business with AI tools she learned in our program.

We do this for the 58-year-old veteran who was told the workforce had passed him by and just built his first digital portfolio and landed a contract.

We do this for the 71-year-old grandmother who FaceTimed her grandkids for the first time. And cried. Not because the technology was hard. Because nobody had ever taken the time to show her.

Those moments don't fit on a spreadsheet. But they are the only metrics that matter.

---

## The Real Definition

Influence is not who you know.

It's not the conferences you attend. It's not the panels you sit on. It's not the newsletters you subscribe to or the thought leaders you repost.

Influence is one human being standing in front of another human being and saying, "I know this feels overwhelming. I've been there too. Let me show you."

Influence is the grandmother who learns AI on Tuesday and teaches her neighbor on Thursday.

Influence is the retired teacher who joins our program and then volunteers to facilitate sessions at her local library.

Influence is the 55-year-old who was laid off, learned to build an AI-powered business plan in our workshop, and is now employing two people.

That's influence. That's what it actually looks like. Not a network. Not a portfolio. Not a follower count.

People. Helping people. One at a time. Until it's 50,000. And then it's a million. And then it's just the way things work.
