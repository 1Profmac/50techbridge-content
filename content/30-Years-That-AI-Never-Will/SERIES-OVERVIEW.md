## Core Messaging Principles

### 1. Make the Big Numbers Personal

When we cite the $850 billion or $76 trillion, we don't leave it as a market stat:

> **"Some of that $850 billion is your money."**

The $850B is the reader's 401(k), their consulting rate, their severance, their seat at the table. The $76T is their retirement, their equity, their earning years being discounted. Every big number gets brought home to YOUR career, YOUR income, YOUR future.

### 2. Experience Is the Input, Not the Obstacle

The series reframes the entire AI conversation:
- Old frame: "Can I learn AI at my age?"
- New frame: "My 30 years of experience is what makes AI work."

### 3. Every Article Ends With Action

No article ends with inspiration. Every article ends with a specific prompt, a specific tool, a specific thing you can do today. The tagline demands it — "applying," not "learning."
