## The 7-Day Plan

You don't need a business plan. You don't need funding. You don't need an MBA. You need a problem, seven days, and the willingness to start ugly.

### Day 1: Name the Problem

Write this sentence and nothing else:

> "I help [who] solve [what specific problem] using [my experience in what]."

30 years of work gave you this sentence. The person who can fill in all three blanks has a business. The person who can't hasn't picked a lane yet — and that's fine. Pick the problem you've solved more times than you can count. The one people always come to you for.

That's the business.

### Day 2: Set Up the Stack

Open accounts for Claude Pro (or ChatGPT Plus), Canva, Google Workspace, and Calendly. That's your Day 1 stack — $53/month. Add the rest as you need them.

Then give your AI its first assignment:

> "I'm starting a consulting business helping [who] with [what]. I have [X] years of experience in [field]. What should my first offer look like? Give me a one-page description with scope, deliverables, timeline, and a price."

Read the output. Edit it with your judgment. You just built your first offer — using exactly what this series has been telling you. You are not learning AI. You are applying AI to what you already know.

### Day 3: Build Your Offer Page

Open Canva or a simple website builder. Create one page with:

- What you do (one sentence)
- Who it's for (one sentence)
- What they get (3-5 bullet points)
- What it costs (one number)
- How to book (Calendly link)

One page. One offer. One price. Don't build a website. Build a door.

### Day 4: Write Your First 3 LinkedIn Posts

Use your AI to draft. Your voice to finish. Three posts:

1. **The insight post:** One thing you learned in 30 years that most people in your industry get wrong.
2. **The proof post:** A specific result you delivered — numbers, outcomes, what changed.
3. **The offer post:** "I'm now helping [who] with [what]. Here's what that looks like."

### Day 5: Post and Reach Out

Publish your first post. Then message 10 people who might need what you offer — not strangers. People who already know your name. Former colleagues, clients, vendors, industry contacts.

Not "just wanted to reach out." A specific message:

> "Hi [Name] — I've started offering [service] for [who]. Given your work at [company], thought this might be relevant. Worth a 15-minute conversation?"

### Day 6: Take Your First Call

Free if needed. The goal isn't revenue on Day 6. The goal is proof — proof that someone will sit across from you and listen to what you're offering. Proof over price this week.

Ask at the end: "Is this something you'd pay for?" Listen to the answer. That's your market research.

### Day 7: Deliver and Repeat

If someone said yes — deliver. Overdeliver. Then ask for a testimonial.

If nobody said yes yet — post again, message 10 more people, take another call. The business is a repetition machine, not a lottery ticket.

**You just launched a business in 7 days for $120/month using 30 years of expertise as the product.**

## Why 42% Started While Still Working

You don't need to quit your job. 42% of entrepreneurs over 50 started their businesses as side projects while still employed. Five to ten hours a week — a Tuesday evening, a Saturday morning — is enough to test whether this works before you make any irreversible decisions.

The AI stack runs while you sleep. The Calendly link books while you're in meetings. The content you posted Tuesday gets engagement all week.

You're not building a startup. You're building a side door — one that becomes the front door when you're ready.
