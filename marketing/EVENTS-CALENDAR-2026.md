These are open to the public. Show up. Be visible. Comment when relevant.

### Workforce Solutions Capital Area Board Meetings
- **When:** Monthly (check website for schedule)
- **Where:** Austin (in-person + virtual option)
- **Why:** **THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT MEETING FOR LMT.** This is the body that approves WIOA training providers in Travis County. Public comment period at every meeting.
- **Action:**
  - Find next meeting on wfscapitalarea.com
  - Attend in person
  - Sign up for public comment (3 minutes)
  - Introduce yourself to board members during break
  - Bring printed capability sheets

- **When:** Thursdays, weekly
- **Where:** Austin City Hall, 301 W 2nd St

- **When:** Monthly

## Quarterly cadence — what Brian commits to

If you do all this, you'll exhaust yourself. Here's the realistic minimum.

### Each month:
- **2 in-person events** (1 civic meeting + 1 chamber/SHRM/professional event)
- **1 free speaking gig** (Rotary, library, senior center, or church)
- **1 application or pitch sent** for a paid speaking spot or conference

### Each quarter:
- **1 large conference attended** (Austin SHRM, Texas EDC, Texas Workforce Conference, NAWB Forum, etc.)
- **1 large conference applied to speak at**
- **1 public comment delivered** at City Council or Workforce Solutions board

### Each year:
- **1 national-tier event** (NAWB Forum, On Aging, Fortune Innovation Summit, USCM)
- **1 published op-ed** (Austin American-Statesman, Texas Tribune, HBR, Fast Company)
- **1 board service application** (workforce board, commission, advisory committee)
