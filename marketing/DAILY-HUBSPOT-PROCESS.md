# Daily HubSpot Process — LMT
## Your morning routine, every day, 30 minutes

---

## 7:30 AM — Open HubSpot (5 min)

1. Open HubSpot → search "Tasks" in the top bar
2. Look at today's tasks — these are your calls, emails, follow-ups
3. Do the tasks. Check them off as you complete each one.
4. After each call or email, click into the deal and click **Note** to log what happened ("Called Jessica, she said X, next step is Y")

---

## 7:45 AM — LinkedIn Morning Hunt (20 min)

1. Open LinkedIn
2. Send **15-20 connection requests** using the search strings from DECISION-MAKER-TARGET-LIST.md
3. When someone **accepts your connection request:**
   - Go to HubSpot → Contacts → Create Contact
   - Add their name, title, company, LinkedIn URL
   - Create a Deal if they're a real buyer
   - Add a Task: "Send intro message on LinkedIn" due tomorrow
4. When you **have a conversation** on LinkedIn:
   - Go to HubSpot → find the contact → click Note
   - Log it: "LinkedIn DM — they said X, I said Y, next step Z"
   - Update the deal stage if it moved

---

## 8:05 AM — Post content (5 min)

Post from the CONTENT-DISTRIBUTION-CHART.md schedule. One post per day.

---

## 12:00 PM — Midday check (5 min)

1. Check LinkedIn notifications — reply to any comments or messages
2. If any new conversations happened, log them in HubSpot (Note on the contact)

---

## 5:00 PM — End of day (5 min)

1. Open HubSpot → Tasks → check off anything you completed today
2. For any task you didn't finish, reschedule it to tomorrow
3. If a deal moved forward today, drag it to the next stage
4. If you met someone new worth tracking, add them as a Contact + Deal

---

## WEEKLY (Friday, 15 min)

1. Open HubSpot → Deals board view
2. Count: How many deals moved forward this week?
3. Count: How many new contacts added?
4. Ask yourself: Is every deal's next action scheduled as a task?
5. If any deal has NO upcoming task, create one right now

---

## THE RULES

1. **Every conversation gets logged.** If it's not in HubSpot, it didn't happen.
2. **Every deal needs a next task.** No deal sits without a scheduled action.
3. **Tasks are single actions.** "Call Jessica" not "Work on AARP partnership."
4. **Check tasks first, LinkedIn second.** HubSpot tells you what to do. LinkedIn is where you do it.
5. **Don't browse.** Open HubSpot, do your tasks, close it. Open LinkedIn, do your hunt, close it.
