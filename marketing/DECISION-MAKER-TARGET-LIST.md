## HOW TO CONNECT ON LINKEDIN (not cold pitching)

**Step 1: Connect with a note (under 300 characters):**
```
Hi [Name] — I lead Learn More Technologies, an MBE-certified workforce training company in Austin focused on adults 50+. I saw your work with [their org] and would love to connect. — Brian McKinney
```

**Step 2: After they accept (wait 1-2 days), send a message:**
```
Thanks for connecting, [Name]. I noticed [specific thing about their work or org]. We train adults 50+ in AI and digital skills — 347 completions, 3X industry completion rate, WIOA-eligible curriculum.

If your team is ever looking for a training partner or speaker, I would love a 15-minute conversation. No pitch — just want to understand what you are working on.

Here is a quick sizzle reel if you are curious: https://youtu.be/EVoRJJJf1Ds
```

**Step 3: If they respond, book a Calendly call. If they don't, engage with their posts for 2 weeks, then follow up once.**

## WHO TO AVOID (tire kickers / lookie-loos)

- Anyone with "aspiring" or "seeking opportunities" in their headline
- People who want to "pick your brain" without a budget
- Organizations that want free workshops with no path to a contract
- Anyone who asks for a proposal before a conversation
- Connections who engage with your content but never convert — that is marketing, not sales

---

## DAILY LINKEDIN ROUTINE (15 minutes)

1. **Send 5-10 connection requests** to people from the search strings above
2. **Comment on 3 posts** from people in your target list (thoughtful comments, not "great post!")
3. **Post or share 1 piece of content** (Short, article, stat, or thought)
4. **Check messages** and respond to any warm leads
5. **Do NOT scroll the feed** beyond your target list

## THE RULE

If they can't write a check, they're not your target. Be friendly to everyone. Spend your time on decision makers.
