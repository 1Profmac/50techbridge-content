## Workflow for each prospect

1. **Research** — run `/prospect [name] [org]` skill
2. **Enrich** — find LinkedIn URL, recent post, mutuals (Apollo / Sales Nav)
3. **Warm** — comment on a recent post OR find a mutual intro
4. **Contact** — pick template T1 / T2 / T3 from `outreach-templates.md`, send
5. **Track** — update Status column, set Next action date
6. **Follow up** — at 5 days, 12 days, 30 days; then move to ⛔ DEAD if no reply

## Weekly cadence
- **Mon AM:** Research 5 new prospects (move 🟦 → 🟨)
- **Tue AM:** Warm 5 prospects (engage on their posts before messaging)
- **Wed AM:** Send 5 outreach messages (move 🟧 → 🟩)
- **Thu AM:** Follow up on stalled threads
- **Fri PM:** Review pipeline, update notes, commit to git

5 contacts/week × 50 weeks = 250 touches/year. With a 5–10% meeting rate, that's 12–25 real conversations per year — enough to land multiple WDB contracts.
