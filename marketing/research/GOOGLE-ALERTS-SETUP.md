## Gmail filter (manual setup — 2 min)

After alerts are live, create this filter so they don't clutter your inbox:

1. Gmail → Settings gear → **See all settings** → **Filters and Blocked Addresses** tab
2. **Create a new filter**
3. **From:** `googlealerts-noreply@google.com`
4. Click **Create filter**
5. Check: **Skip the Inbox (Archive it)** + **Apply the label:** `Research/50+AI` (create it when prompted)
6. Click **Create filter**

**Review rhythm:** Open the `Research/50+AI` label every **Friday morning during the Friday Review block**. Skim 5–10 alerts from the week. Promising items → tell me "add to stats sheet: [item]" and I'll log it to `marketing/research/stats-sheet.md` with citation.

## Pruning rule (read in 30 days)

If an alert has fired zero useful items after 30 days, **kill it**. Replace with:
- A different author from the source
- A different keyword angle
- A different authority source entirely

Info overwhelm kills systems faster than info scarcity. 12 alerts is a ceiling, not a floor.
