- **Why:** HR directors, workforce execs, and L&D leaders search LinkedIn by keyword. Your headline is your 220-character SEO. "I Fight For You" is emotional; recruiters search functional terms.

- **Why:** Buyers qualify you in 3 seconds. Numbers do that faster than mission statements.

After this checklist is complete:
- Profile viewers should jump from ~13/week to 40+/week within 30 days
- Post impressions should jump from 320 to 1,000+ per post within 30 days
- Inbound DMs from buyers (not consultants) should start appearing within 60 days

If not moving: the problem isn't the profile, it's the volume of outreach. Re-audit the Morning Hunt block.

## WHY ALL OF THIS MATTERS

Your LinkedIn profile is the first thing a buyer sees after you send a connection request. If it looks like a résumé for a job seeker, you're positioned as a job seeker. If it looks like a vendor with proof, budget, and authority, you're positioned as a vendor. **Same person. Different frame. Completely different outcome.**

You are not a small fish. Your profile should say so.
