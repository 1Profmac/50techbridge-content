# LinkedIn Services Marketplace — Category Strategy

**Last updated:** 2026-04-07
**Decision:** Focus, not expand. Switch from 2 wrong categories to 3 right ones.

---

## The principle

In a crowded marketplace, **niche beats broad**. There are tens of thousands of generic "Business Consultants" and "Career Coaches" on LinkedIn — a new provider ranks on page 47. Narrow categories targeted at workforce/HR buyers have far fewer providers — you can rank page 1 if you focus.

LinkedIn Services Marketplace ranks providers by:
1. Category match (specific keywords win)
2. Response time (slow replies hurt visibility)
3. Reviews and completed requests
4. Profile completeness

Brian's leverage: pick categories where his ICP (workforce decision makers, HR leaders, MBE buyers, event organizers) actually search, then dominate the smaller pool.

## REMOVE these categories

| Category | Why remove |
|---|---|
| ❌ Career Development Coaching | Wrong audience — attracts individual job seekers, not workforce decision makers. The 3 inbound leads from this category were wrong-fit. |
| ❌ Business Consulting | Too generic. Competing with everyone. Won't rank. Wastes marketplace ranking signal. |

## ADD these 3 categories (the right lineup)

### #1 — Corporate Training (HIGHEST PRIORITY)
- **Why:** The exact search workforce/HR/L&D leaders use when they need a training vendor
- **Who searches it:** HR leaders, L&D managers, workforce board staff, training coordinators
- **LMT fit:** Direct — LMT IS a corporate training provider with proof (3X completion, 347 Pioneers, 23 partner orgs)
- **Search volume:** Medium-high
- **Competition:** Moderate, but MBE + age niche differentiates

### #2 — Diversity & Inclusion Consulting (HIGHEST DIFFERENTIATION)
- **Why:** Combines MBE certification + age-inclusion message — almost nobody competes here
- **Who searches it:** DEI leads at large employers, HR equity officers, MBE supplier diversity managers
- **LMT fit:** Strongest competitive moat — "AI training designed for adults 50+" is a narrow, ownable D&I angle
- **Search volume:** Lower but higher conversion
- **Competition:** Low for the age-inclusion sub-niche

### #3 — Public Speaking (REVENUE PATH)
- **Why:** Where conferences and event organizers find speakers. Direct line to speaking-gig revenue ($2.5K-10K/event)
- **Who searches it:** Event organizers, conference programmers, chamber executives, association leaders
- **LMT fit:** Growing — $850B Series gives speaker reel material
- **Search volume:** High
- **Competition:** High, but contrarian-data angle is differentiated
