## Revenue targets to fund each tier

| Tier | Monthly cost | Required monthly revenue (3X cost rule) |
|---|---|---|
| Lean | $220 | $660 (1 consulting call/mo @ $200 + 1 small workshop @ $500) |
| Growth | $900 | $2,700 (1 small contract + 1 speaking gig + 2 consulting calls) |
| Aggressive | $2,500 | $7,500 (1 large contract OR 2 mid contracts OR 3 speaking gigs) |

**The 3X rule:** Marketing budget should be ~33% of revenue, max. If you're spending more than 1/3 of revenue on marketing, the math doesn't work long-term.

## What NOT to spend money on

- ❌ **Generic email blast tools** (don't pay for ZoomInfo / Apollo / lead lists — bad ROI for B2B relationship sales)
- ❌ **SEO agencies** (do it yourself with the morning routine + content engine)
- ❌ **PR agencies** (pitch op-eds yourself; agencies cost $3K-10K/mo with no guarantees)
- ❌ **Branding refresh** (your brand is fine; don't waste money on a logo redesign)
- ❌ **TikTok production** (wrong audience for B2B workforce)
- ❌ **Conference booths under 500 attendees** (waste of $)
- ❌ **Virtual summits as a sponsor** (low ROI unless you're presenting)
- ❌ **Pay-to-play awards** (ones that charge you to be considered)

---

## Hidden costs to plan for

- **Time:** Every hour Brian spends on marketing has an opportunity cost. Track it.
- **Travel deposits:** Conferences require booking 3-6 months out
- **Tax on speaking fees:** Self-employment tax + state tax
- **Equipment depreciation:** Camera, mic, lighting all need replacement eventually
- **Bookkeeping:** As revenue grows, you'll need a real accountant ($300-500/quarter)
- **Legal:** First contract review, trademark of "50+TechBridge", etc. (~$500-1,000 one-time)
