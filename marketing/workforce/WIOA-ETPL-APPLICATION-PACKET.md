## Why this packet exists

LMT serves adults 50+ with AI/digital training. We have proof: 347 Pioneers, 3X completion rate, 74% confidence increase, 23 partner organizations.

Right now, LMT runs without WIOA funding. Pioneers come for free; LMT absorbs the cost.

Once LMT is on the **Texas Eligible Training Provider List (ETPL)**, individual job seekers and dislocated workers using WIOA Title I funding can choose LMT and the local Workforce Development Board pays LMT directly per enrollee.

**Revenue model:** WIOA training payments range **$1,500 to $8,000 per enrollee** depending on program length and outcomes. 20 funded Pioneers/quarter = **$30K-160K/quarter** new revenue.

**This is the highest-leverage business development application LMT can submit in 2026.** Treat it like a contract pitch.

## Required Certifications, Licenses, and Compliance Requirements

**This section is the most likely cause of application rejection. Get these right.**

### 1. Texas Career Schools and Colleges (CSC) Authorization — REQUIRED

Texas requires every training provider to fall under one of three categories:

| Path | Who qualifies | What you need |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) CSC Licensed** | For-profit and nonprofit career schools offering occupational training | Apply through TWC CSC program. License fee, financial bond, facility inspection, curriculum approval. ~$2,000-5,000 + 4-6 month process. |
| **(b) CSC Exempt** | Programs that meet specific exemptions in Texas Education Code Chapter 132 (e.g., short courses under 40 hours, free programs, certain nonprofit programs, trade association training) | Request Letter of Exemption from TWC CSC. Free. ~30-day process. |
| **(c) Accredited** | Programs accredited by a U.S. Department of Education recognized accrediting body (regional or national accreditor, e.g., ACCET, COE, DEAC) | Submit accreditation letter + scope of accreditation. |

**LMT's likely path:** **(b) Exemption** under Texas Education Code §132.002 — short non-credit courses or community-based training programs are typically exempt. **MUST CONFIRM** with TWC CSC before submitting WIOA ETPL application.

**Action this week:**
- Email TWC CSC at career.schools@twc.texas.gov OR call (512) 936-3100
- Subject: *"Exemption status inquiry — Learn More Technologies adult digital skills program"*
- Body: Describe the program (length in hours, fee structure, target population, MBE certification) and ask which exemption category applies. Request a written exemption letter if eligible.
- Expect 2-4 weeks to receive the letter

## Texas Career Schools and Colleges Question (CRITICAL)

Texas requires training providers to either:
- (a) Be **licensed** by the Texas Workforce Commission Career Schools and Colleges (CSC) program, OR
- (b) Have an **exemption letter** from CSC, OR
- (c) Be **accredited** by a recognized accrediting body

**LMT action:** Email TWC CSC directly to determine which path applies.
- **TWC Career Schools and Colleges:** career.schools@twc.texas.gov | (512) 936-3100
- **Question to ask:** *"Our organization provides short-form digital skills and AI training for adults 50+. We're applying to be a WIOA ETPL training provider. Do we need a Career Schools and Colleges license, or do we qualify for an exemption letter?"*

This is a **gating question** — without resolving (a)/(b)/(c), the WIOA ETPL application will be rejected.

## Risks and red flags

- **CSC license/exemption is a hard gate.** Don't skip step 1 of week 1.
- **Letters of support from partners take time.** Ask now.
- **Performance data must be verifiable.** "3X completion rate" needs to be backed up by enrollment + completion records. Have these ready.
- **Workforce dollars come with strings.** Federal audit rules apply. Don't take this on without bookkeeping support.
- **The reviewer is human.** Tone matters. Be professional, data-driven, not overpromising.
- **WIOA is political.** Don't lead with ageism rhetoric in the application — lead with workforce ROI and measurable outcomes.
