# LMT Outreach Templates

**Owner:** Brian McKinney
**Use with:** `wioa-prospects.md` pipeline, LinkedIn DMs / InMails, follow-up emails
**Last updated:** 2026-04-07

## Rules of the road

1. **Never message anyone you haven't researched.** Run `/prospect [name]` first.
2. **Never message anyone you haven't warmed.** Comment on their post or find a mutual first.
3. **Three templates only.** Don't write 50 variants — these three cover 95% of cases.
4. **One ask, one sentence.** Every message ends with one specific 15-minute call request.
5. **Send Tuesday–Thursday 8–10 AM CT.** Highest LinkedIn open rates.
6. **Follow up at 5 days, 12 days, 30 days.** Then mark ⛔ DEAD.

---

## T1 — Cold but Warmed (you commented on their post first)

**When to use:** No mutual connection, but you engaged with something they posted in the last 7 days.

```
Hi [Name] — your post on [topic] last week stuck with me, especially the line about [specific phrase from their post]. It mirrors something I see every week running Learn More Technologies (50+TechBridge): a WIOA-eligible AI fluency program for adults 50+. We're MBE-certified in Texas with 347+ Pioneers trained at a 3X industry completion rate.

I'd love 15 minutes of your time to learn how [their org] thinks about [specific challenge they posted about]. No pitch — just a real conversation between two people working on the same problem from different angles.

Open to a quick call next week?

— Brian
```

**Why it works:**
- Opens with their content (proves you actually read it)
- Quotes a specific phrase (impossible to fake)
- Frames LMT in one sentence with proof (347+, 3X, MBE)
- Asks for 15 min, not 30 — lower friction
- "No pitch" disarms sales-fatigue
- Ends with a clear yes/no question

## T2 — Mutual Intro (someone connected you)

**When to use:** A mutual connection mentioned them, introduced you, or you can credibly name-drop.

```
Hi [Name] — [Mutual]'s name came up when I was asking around about [topic / state ETPL / WDB approval / etc.], and they pointed me your way. So now I'm here.

Quick context: I run Learn More Technologies (50+TechBridge), an MBE-certified, WIOA-eligible AI fluency program built specifically for adults 50+. 347+ Pioneers trained, 3X industry completion rate, designed around the gaps your [adult program / older worker program / business services] clients keep running into.

[Mutual] thought it might be worth us having a 15-minute conversation about [specific ask: ETPL listing / WDB vendor approval / co-delivery / etc.]. Could I grab a slot on your calendar next week?

— Brian
```

**Why it works:**
- Names the mutual immediately (highest trust signal)
- "So now I'm here" is human, not corporate
- Leads with the ask in concrete language
- Doesn't dump credentials — uses three numbers and stops
- Ends with the calendar request, not a vague "let me know"

## T3 — Event Follow-up (you actually met them)

**When to use:** You shook their hand, were on the same panel, attended the same conference, or were in the same Zoom room.

```
Hi [Name] — Brian McKinney here. We were both at [event] last [week / month] — I was the one [identifying detail: speaking on the workforce panel / asking the question about WIOA eligibility / sitting next to you at the keynote].

Your point about [specific thing they said] is actually exactly what I'm building toward at Learn More Technologies (50+TechBridge) — a WIOA-eligible AI fluency program for adults 50+, MBE-certified, 347+ Pioneers, 3X completion rate.

Would love 15 minutes to keep the conversation going. I have a specific question about [specific ask] I think you're the right person to answer. Open to a quick call next week?

— Brian
```

**Why it works:**
- Reactivates the in-person memory immediately
- Identifying detail proves you were actually there
- Quotes their words back to them
- Frames LMT as the natural extension of *their* point
- Ends with a specific question they're flattered to answer

## Follow-up cadence (use the same templates, edited)

**Day 5 — gentle nudge**
> Hi [Name] — bumping this up in case it got buried. Still would love 15 minutes whenever you have a spot. — Brian

**Day 12 — value-add nudge**
> Hi [Name] — thought of you when [thing happened: a relevant article, a new piece of research, a Part 3 of the $850B series I just shipped]. Sharing in case it's useful: [link]. Same offer stands on the call.

**Day 30 — final nudge with permission**
> Hi [Name] — I don't want to clutter your inbox. If now isn't the right time, no worries at all. Mind if I check back in 90 days? — Brian

After Day 30 with no reply → mark ⛔ DEAD in `wioa-prospects.md`. Move on. Re-engage in 6 months with a new angle.

---

## What NOT to do

- ❌ Don't open with "Hope you're doing well" — wasted line
- ❌ Don't say "I'd love to learn more about you and your work" — vague, no ask
- ❌ Don't paste a 4-paragraph LMT description — three numbers max
- ❌ Don't ask for "30 minutes" — ask for 15
- ❌ Don't send on Friday afternoon or Monday morning — Tue–Thu AM only
- ❌ Don't use emojis in cold WIOA outreach — keep it boardroom-clean
- ❌ Don't mention pricing in the first message — that's the call's job
- ❌ Don't use the words "synergy," "leverage," "circle back," or "touch base"
