## Engagement Rules

### The 60-minute rule
Every comment on YOUR post gets a reply within 60 minutes if at all possible. The first hour decides reach.

### The 3-comment rule
Before posting your own content, comment on 3 other people's posts in the last 24 hours. Train the algorithm that you're an active member, not a broadcaster.

### The 5-pattern rule
Every reply uses one of the 5 patterns from the morning routine (follow-up question, quote+add, tag, respectful disagree, never emoji-only).

### The "give before ask" rule
For every 10 posts, 7-8 should be insight/value and 2-3 can be CTA/promotion. If your feed is all promotion, people unfollow.
