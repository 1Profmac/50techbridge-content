# B-Roll Prompts — Chunk 3: What Winning Organizations Do Differently

**Canva specs:** 1920×1080 landscape, navy bg `#0E1C2F`, gold accents `#C8942E`, 6–8 seconds each, no text baked in.
**Talent rule:** Adults 50+ shown as professional, capable, engaged. Never "elderly," "struggling," or stereotyped. No chromakey.

### clip-3-02
Over-the-shoulder shot of a man in his early 60s working through an interactive tablet lesson at a clean desk. Hands moving deliberately on the screen, focused expression. Soft window light.

Side-by-side close-up of two pairs of hands at a workbench: experienced hands and younger hands working together on the same component or interface. No faces yet — emphasize collaboration and craft.

Wide shot of a small training cohort of adults 50+ in a bright, open workspace, laughing during a hands-on group exercise. NOT a lecture hall. Energy is curiosity, not instruction.

## SEGMENT 4 — The Urgency Is Not Going Away
*Narration beats:* 1 in 5 over 65 by 2030 · 10,000 Boomers/day · 2.1M manufacturing vacancies · 50% utility retirements · $850B GDP loss · $28.2T by 2050

### clip-3-13
Wide aerial or rooftop shot of a busy American downtown at the start of a workday — commuters streaming into office buildings. Implies scale, demographic momentum, "this is happening now."

### clip-3-14
Empty hard hats hanging on a row of pegs in an industrial facility locker room. Some occupied, several empty. Quiet, suggestive of the workforce cliff. No dialogue, no people in frame.

### clip-3-15
A control room or utility operations center: monitors glowing, one or two operators visible — but conspicuously few. Mid-shot, slow push-in. Conveys understaffed critical infrastructure.

### clip-3-16
A confident 50+ professional standing in a modern office or training space, looking directly toward camera (not at it), arms relaxed. Held shot, calm dignity. This is the closing beat — "the workforce you're ignoring is ready."
