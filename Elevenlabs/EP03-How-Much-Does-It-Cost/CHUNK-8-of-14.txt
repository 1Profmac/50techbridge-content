But she was running out of runway. Severance was going to last another four months. So she decided to try.

Here's exactly what Sandra spent:

Month one. She signed up for a free Claude account. Free. Zero dollars. She used it to clarify her service offering. She had twenty-two years of project management experience, but she didn't know how to package that for the market. She sat down with Claude and had a conversation. Not a Google search. A conversation.

She asked: "I'm a PMP-certified project manager with twenty-two years of experience. What kind of consulting services could I offer to small nonprofits?"

And Claude helped her map out three service tiers. A project audit, a one-time assessment of how an organization runs its projects. A twelve-week implementation engagement where she'd set up project management systems. And an ongoing fractional Chief of Staff role.

That conversation alone was worth thousands of dollars in consulting. Sandra got it for free, sitting at her kitchen table on a Tuesday morning.

Week two. She set up a WordPress website. Twelve dollars a month for the basic business plan. She used Claude to write every word on that site. Homepage. About page. Services page. Contact page. She bought a domain name, fourteen dollars for the year. The whole site was live in a weekend.

Was it perfect? No. Was it professional enough to send to a potential client? Yes. And that's the bar. That's the only bar that matters when you're starting.
