## CLIP 01 — The March
**Filename:** `clip-01-the-march.mp4`
**Scene timing:** 0:03 – 0:15 (12 seconds)
**Voiceover over this clip:** "What if things were different."

```
Wide angle futuristic corporate lobby interior, low camera angle looking up at crowd.
Dozens of businesspeople in dark navy and grey suits walking in the same direction
past the camera, all identical in pace and posture, all facing forward. Blue-tinted
fluorescent overhead lighting, enormous glass walls, polished dark marble floors.
Slight atmospheric haze backlit from floor-to-ceiling windows behind the crowd.
Workers silhouetted and motion-blurred in slow motion — indistinct, interchangeable.
One Black man age 63 is visible among them, his face turned slightly toward camera.
His eyes are distant, present but elsewhere. Oppressive, institutional. Near-future
aesthetic, not sci-fi — just 5 years from now and already cold. Ridley Scott
cinematic quality. Shot on 35mm, anamorphic lens, lens flare from backlight.
```

**Filename:** `clip-02-algorithm-interview.mp4`
**Scene timing:** 0:15 – 0:28 (13 seconds)

```
Near-future corporate interview room, sleek minimalist design, cold blue-white

meet his eyes. Slight desaturation of skin tones. The injustice is procedural,
not dramatic. Cinematic. Slow motion push toward the screen. Tense silence implied.
```

## CLIP 03 — The Kiosk She Cannot Navigate
**Filename:** `clip-03-kiosk-fail.mp4`
**Scene timing:** 0:28 – 0:38 (10 seconds)
**Voiceover:** "What if the systems built to serve you were actually built with you in mind..."

```
Near-future pharmacy interior, clean white and chrome surfaces. Automated dispensers
behind glass, robotic arms visible. A Black woman late 60s with silver locs stands
at a sleek white touchscreen kiosk. The kiosk screen shows tiny interface icons
with no text labels. She touches the screen once, uncertain, then again. She steps
back in frustration. Her hands lower. Cold white LED lighting. The automated
pharmacy continues operating behind her, indifferent. She turns and walks out of
frame without completing her transaction. The kiosk blinks, processing nothing
about her. Her exit is quiet and undramatic — that is what makes it devastating.
```

## CLIP 04 — The Authority Screen
**Filename:** `clip-04-boardroom-screen.mp4`
**Scene timing:** 0:38 – 0:50 (12 seconds)
**Voiceover:** "What if technology wasn't developed by 25-year-olds for 25-year-olds..."

```
Massive corporate boardroom, futuristic near-future design. Floor-to-ceiling
projection wall spanning the entire front of the room. On the wall-screen in
large clean sans-serif data font: "TARGET DEMOGRAPHIC" / "AGE: 25–40" — centered,
stark, white text on dark background. A presenter stands nearly silhouetted before
it, small against the enormous display. Young executives in tailored suits sit
around a curved dark table — nobody raises a hand, nobody questions. Slow camera
push toward the screen. The numbers grow larger. Cold blue light from above.
Shot from behind the executives looking toward the screen. The image feels like
physics, not people — a law being read, not a decision being made. Cinematic,
dystopian, oppressive weight.
```

**Filename:** `clip-06-three-workers.mp4`
**Scene timing:** 1:02 – 1:12 (10 seconds)
**Voiceover:** "...instead of using it to replace them."

```
Back of a corporate conference room. Three workers in medium foreground shot:
a Black woman age 52, a Latino man age 58, a white man age 61 — all in business
dress, all looking forward at a presentation screen ahead of them. The 58-year-old
man is in sharpest focus. His face: not afraid. Tired. The specific exhaustion of
a person who has read this room before and already knows what the numbers mean
when they point at him. He does not look surprised. He looks like someone who has
learned not to be. Slow motion. Cold screen light from ahead of them illuminates
their faces in blue-white. The presentation screen is visible but out of focus
behind them — its content implied, not shown. 8 seconds.
```

## CLIP 08 — The Real Smile
**Filename:** `clip-08-real-smile.mp4`
**Scene timing:** 1:15 – 1:28 (13 seconds)
**Voiceover:** "We asked that question. Every version of it. And then we stopped waiting for an answer."

```
Community center interior, Austin Texas. Real working space — folding tables, basic
laptops, warm fluorescent light. Not staged. A Black woman age 64 with reading
glasses sits at a table with a laptop open showing an AI chat interface. She types
something carefully. Waits 2 seconds. Reads the response. Her face changes: first
surprise, then something private and warm — the real smile that comes from
understanding something you didn't expect to understand, from realizing you are
more capable than someone else's story about you. It is not polite. It is not for
the camera. She types again, faster. Warm window light from the left. Medium close-up.
Laptop screen glow warm on her face. The room is real, not beautiful. 12 seconds.
```

**Filename:** `clip-10-reversed-interview.mp4`

```
Corporate interview room — warmer version of clip-02. Same type of room but natural

across from a young interviewer. This time the interviewer is leaning forward,
```
