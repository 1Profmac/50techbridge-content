# Working the Warm Signal Stack

**LMT Operating Principle**
**Owner:** Brian McKinney
**Created:** April 20, 2026
**Status:** Active — applies to all platforms
**Trigger Context:** Phase 1B — "Come Down From the Clouds" · Revenue is Priority 1

---

## The Principle

**When a platform shows engagement on our work, the response is not optional. It is strategic execution.**

Volume is not strategy. Targeting is.

We do not improvise outreach. We work warm signals through a defined sequence — every time.

---

## Why This Exists

Most LinkedIn (and platform) outreach fails because operators treat reactions, comments, and DMs as noise to scroll past. They are not noise. They are pre-qualified hands raised by people who have already self-selected by engaging with our work.

Cold outreach to strangers converts at 1–3%. Warm outreach to people who already engaged with our content converts at 20–40%.

**The warm signal stack is the highest-leverage outreach available to LMT — and it is free.**

---

## The 5-Step Sequence

### 1. Triage

Read every signal before responding. Sort by:

| Signal Strength | What It Looks Like |
|---|---|
| 🔴 Hot | Substantive comment + repeat engagement (multiple touches across multiple posts) |
| 🟠 Warm | Substantive comment OR multiple reactions over time |
| 🟡 Light | Single reaction · no comment |
| ⚪ Skip | Anonymous "+1 other" reactors with no name visible |

**Rule:** Work hot first. Work warm second. Batch the lights.

---

### 2. Profile-First

Never message anyone without 60 seconds on their profile first.

What changes the message:
- Headline & tagline (their positioning)
- Banner imagery (often reveals book launches, programs, or trademarked methods)
- Follower count (signals whether they're peer-grade or follower-grade)
- Credentials (PhD, MSc, founder, ex-major-company)
- Pronoun field (often contains brand voice — e.g., "She/Her/Arsonist")
- "Featured In" press logos
- Mutual connections

**Rule:** A peer with 30K followers and a book launch gets a peer-grade DM. A casual reactor gets a connect request. Treatment is determined by tier — not by who messaged whom first.

---

### 3. Personalize the Open

One acknowledgment of their actual work earns more replies than ten "thanks for the reaction" templates.

**The acknowledgment formula:**

> [Specific work / brand IP / book / position] + [why it matters] + [how it intersects with our mission]

**Example (Dr. Angela Mulrooney):**
> "'Arsonist' is my favorite pronoun on LinkedIn this year, and 'You Are Not Irrelevant' is a title I wish I'd written. We're clearly fighting the same fight — you with accomplished professionals, me with adults 50+ at LMT."

**Example (Katheline Jean-Pierre):**
> "Coming from someone who actually ran LinkedIn at Google and built The Top 5% Method®, that meant something."

**Rule:** If you can't name something specific from their work, you have not done step 2 properly. Go back.

---

### 4. Lead With a Gift

Offer before asking. The gift must be:

- **Concrete** — name a specific thing, not "let me know how I can help"
- **Costless to us** — Pioneer access is already free, content amplification is reach we already have
- **Valuable to them** — something they can give to their audience or clients

**Standard gifts available from LMT:**

| Gift | Cost to Us | Value to Them |
|---|---|---|
| Comp'd Pioneer access for their referrals | $0 | They look generous to clients |
| Personal onboarding for any referral | ~10 min | White-glove differentiator |
| Content amplification to our network | $0 | Reach |
| Cross-promotion / podcast guest swap | Time only | Audience expansion |
| 50+TechBridge data sharing for their research | $0 | Original data they can cite |
| **The Collab Offer (Phase 2 — deploy after first signed contract)** | 1 hour | Co-created LinkedIn Live or recorded session where Brian demos AI visual storytelling stack using THEIR content as the example. They get a finished asset. Brian gets exposure to their audience. Both post it. **Only for T1 peers with 10K+ followers whose audience = HR leaders, workforce directors, L&D. NOT for peers whose audience is coaches/solopreneurs/job seekers.** |

**Rule:** The gift is not a pitch wrapped in nice language. It must be a thing they can say yes or no to without thinking.

---

### 5. Pace By Tier

Don't blow high-leverage moves on light signals.

| Tier | Signal | Outreach Type | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1 Peer** | Substantive comment + 10K+ followers + credentials | Personalized InMail with gift + meeting ask | InMail credit |
| **T2 Engaged** | Substantive comment OR multi-touch reactor | Connect request with personalized note | Free |
| **T3 Reactor** | Single reaction | Connect request with one-line note | Free |
| **T4 Anonymous** | "+1 other" with no name | Skip — can't personalize | N/A |

**Pacing rules:**
- Maximum **3 T1 peer DMs per day** — three is intentional outreach, four is desperation energy
- Burn InMail credits on T1 only (15/month — ration accordingly)
- T2 and T3 work in batches — knock out 5 in a 20-min block
- After sending a T1, pause 30+ minutes before the next T1 — gives mental space to evaluate the next profile properly

## When to Activate This Principle

This principle applies whenever Brian sees:

- Notification of comments on LMT or Brian's posts
- Notification of reactions on LMT or Brian's posts
- New connection requests with notes
- DMs from people not yet in the warm-thread tracker
- Mentions of LMT or Brian on any platform
- Reposts or shares of LMT content

**Trigger phrase:** *"Work the warm stack"* — open Notifications → "My posts" tab and run the 5-step sequence.

## What This Principle Replaces

This principle replaces the following weaker patterns we will no longer use:

| ❌ Old pattern | ✅ New pattern |
|---|---|
| Mass-DM all reactors with the same template | Triage by signal + profile, then personalize |
| "Thanks for the reaction!" replies | Acknowledge specific work + plant a meaningful intersection |
| Skip reactors who "only reacted" | Re-tier based on profile credentials, not signal alone |
| Cold-DM strangers from search | Work the warm signal stack first — always |
| "Let me know how I can help" | Name a specific gift up front |
| Burn InMail credits on first available high-follower account | Pace T1 outreach to ≤3/day, ration credits to 15/month |

## Tracking & Continuity

Every warm signal worked is logged in the **LinkedIn Warm Thread Tracker** with:

- Date sent
- Channel (DM / InMail / Connect)
- Subject line
- Strategic value rating (T1–T4)
- Gift offered
- Next-touch date (default: 7 days if no reply)
- Outcome (no reply · replied · meeting booked · contract · closed)

**Treat every warm signal like the door it actually is.**
