The venture capital myth says you need money to make money. That you need to raise a seed round, find investors, burn through cash for two years, and pray for a Series A.

That is one way to build a business. It is not the only way. And for what we're talking about, a knowledge-based, service-driven, expertise-powered online business, it's not even the right way.

Now here's the part that might surprise you. Being cost-conscious? That thing you feel a little embarrassed about? That is actually your superpower.

If you're over fifty, you've probably lived through at least two recessions. You've watched companies burn through money like it was kindling. You know what it feels like when the layoffs hit because somebody in a corner office wasn't paying attention to the numbers.

That financial discipline, that instinct that says "I'm not spending a dollar until I know what it's buying me," that is the mindset that builds businesses that survive. The startups with the ping pong tables and the cold brew on tap? Half of them are gone in eighteen months.

You're built to last. Let's make sure your startup costs reflect that.
