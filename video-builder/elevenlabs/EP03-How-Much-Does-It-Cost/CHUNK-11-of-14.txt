Alright. Let's lay it out. The Under One Hundred Dollar Starter Stack. Pen and paper time. Or open your Notes app. Whatever generation you are.

Tool number one. Your AI assistant. Claude from Anthropic. The free tier gives you access to one of the most capable AI systems on the planet. You can use it for brainstorming, writing, research, strategy, and problem-solving. If you want more capacity and access to advanced features, the Pro plan is twenty dollars a month. My recommendation: start free. Upgrade when you're making money. Zero to twenty dollars a month.

Tool number two. Your website. WordPress dot com, business plan, twelve dollars a month. This gets you a custom domain, professional themes, and enough functionality to run a legitimate consulting business. You don't need Squarespace. You don't need Wix. You don't need a custom-built site. You need a clean, professional online presence that tells people who you are, what you do, and how to hire you. Twelve dollars a month.

Tool number three. Your email list. Mailchimp free tier. Five hundred contacts. One thousand emails per month. This is your most valuable asset after your expertise. Social media platforms can change their algorithm tomorrow and your reach goes to zero. Your email list belongs to you. Start building it from day one. Zero dollars a month.

Tool number four. Your social media presence. LinkedIn. Free. Full stop. This is where your clients are, especially if you're in professional services, consulting, B2B, nonprofit work, or any knowledge-based field. You don't need to be on Instagram. You don't need to make TikToks. You need to be on LinkedIn, posting valuable content consistently. Zero dollars a month.

Tool number five. Your payment system. Stripe. Free to create an account. Free to set up. They take two point nine percent plus thirty cents per transaction, but only when you get paid. That means it costs you nothing until money is coming in. You can send professional invoices, accept credit cards, and set up recurring billing for retainer clients. Zero dollars a month until you're generating revenue. And then it's a tiny percentage of money you wouldn't have had otherwise.
