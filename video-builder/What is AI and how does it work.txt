Love this concept — it's a brilliant way to make AI tangible for adults 50+. The morph metaphor lands the abstract idea ("it's like talking to a person, but it's software") in one image.

Quick reality check on the tools, then the script:

**Canva and Giphy can't do a true morph.** A real morph (face dissolving into device, features melting between forms) requires After Effects, Runway, or similar. What you *can* do in Canva:

- **Cross-dissolve transition** between a device shot and a person shot (looks like a gentle fade, not a true morph)
- **Cut on action** — device "speaks," cut to person speaking the same line, cut back to device
- **Canva's AI video features** (Magic Animate, Text-to-Video) can generate short clips but morphing between two specific subjects isn't reliable yet

**Better cheat for this concept:** Use **Runway ML** (free tier available) or **Kling AI** for the actual morph shots — 2-3 short clips, then assemble in Canva. Total cost: free or ~$15.

Now — the script. Designed so the morphs happen at natural conversational beats, and so it works even if your "morph" ends up being a cross-dissolve:

**Production notes for Canva:**

The two morph moments (0:20–0:35 and 0:50–1:10) are the only shots that need fancy work. Everything else is standard footage you can shoot on a phone and edit in Canva.

For the morphs, your easiest path:
1. Shoot the device shot and the person shot from the **same angle, same distance, same lighting**
2. In Canva, place them on adjacent timeline clips
3. Apply the **Dissolve** or **Liquid** transition (3-4 seconds long)
4. The slow dissolve *reads* as a morph to most viewers — the brain fills in the rest

If you want a true morph for those two moments, generate them in **Runway ML** (free tier) using the device image and person image as start/end frames, then drop the resulting clips into Canva.
