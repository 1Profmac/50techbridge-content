## THE BLOCKER THAT UNLOCKS EVERYTHING: 501(c)(3) FISCAL SPONSOR

6+ opportunities require nonprofit status. LMT is for-profit LLC. You need a **fiscal sponsor** — a nonprofit that applies on your behalf and passes funds through.

**Candidates to approach:**
- Dr. Shantana Robinson's network (MFAD Creative — ask about LiftFund connection)
- Austin Area Urban League
- Greater Austin Black Chamber (if they have a foundation arm)
- Any nonprofit that's hosted your workshops and has 501(c)(3) status

**The ask:** "Would your organization be willing to serve as fiscal sponsor for a grant application? LMT delivers the training, your org holds the grant. Standard fiscal sponsor fee is 5-10%."
