## 💰 PART 1 — Where the funding is

Concrete dollar amounts, grant names, and who controls them. Sorted by relevance to LMT.

### Tier A — Direct bullseye for 50+TechBridge

| Funding source | Amount | Controlled by | How LMT plays |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AARP Foundation Digital Skills Ready@50+™** | **$10M Google.org grant (2022, ongoing)** | AARP Foundation + Older Adults Technology Services (OATS from AARP) | Become a community partner. This program literally exists to fund digital skills training for adults 50+ with low income. Direct vehicle. |
| **AARP Foundation BACK TO WORK 50+** | Active community-partner network | AARP Foundation | Local community partners deliver short-term training and work-based learning. LMT could become a Texas hub. Phone for partnerships: 1-855-850-2525 |
| **SCSEP (Senior Community Service Employment Program)** | **$312M annual federal pool**, $46.9M to AARP Foundation, $45.4M to CWI Works | DOL → state grantees → community providers | TWC runs SCSEP in 82 TX counties, partnered with AARP Foundation. LMT becomes a training provider in the AARP/TWC pipeline. |

### Tier B — Indirect but addressable

| Funding source | Amount | Controlled by | How LMT plays |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WIOA Title I Adult State Grants** | **$875.6M nationally in FY26** (signed Feb 3, 2026) | DOL → states → local WDBs (28 in TX) → ITAs to providers | Get on the Texas ETPL list. Hope Soliz / Yael Lawson / Jason Helmick are the gatekeepers. |
| **WIOA Title I Dislocated Worker** | Separate state allotment, similar scale | Same chain as Adult | 50+ workers laid off from manufacturing, retail, healthcare = exact LMT target |
| **DOL Industry-Driven Skills Training Fund** | **Wisconsin won $7.3M for advanced manufacturing + AI** in 2026; 14 states received grants | DOL competitive grant program | Watch for next round. Texas should apply. AI-focused = LMT angle. |
| **WIOA Title V — Older Workers** (formal name varies) | Carved out within WIOA for 55+ | Local WDBs | Sub-allocation specifically for the 55+ cohort. Most boards under-spend this. |

## 🎯 STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS

### What this all means for LMT

1. **Stop chasing individual WDBs one at a time.** The actual leverage points are:
   - AARP Foundation partnership (national scale)
   - Texas state ETPL listing (statewide scale)
   - C2GPS partnership (multi-state contractor scale)
   - One named foundation grant (Google.org, Lumina, JPMorgan)

2. **Hope and Yael at WSCA are still the right Austin entry point**, but they should be the *case study* you build *before* approaching AARP Foundation. "We've trained 347 in Austin with WSCA" is the proof point that gets the AARP meeting.

3. **The fastest path to scale is the AARP/OATS partnership.** Carly Roszkowski's team has $10M from Google.org explicitly to fund providers like LMT. Most "providers" they fund are non-AI-focused. LMT walks in with the AI angle and immediately stands out.

4. **The contractor channel (C2GPS, ResCare, Equus, Eckerd) is faster than the WDB channel** because contractors are actively shopping for new training partners, while WDBs are slower-moving public agencies.

5. **You don't need WIOA Title I to start.** SCSEP, AARP Foundation, and direct foundation grants don't require ETPL listing. Pursue those *while* the ETPL paperwork grinds.
