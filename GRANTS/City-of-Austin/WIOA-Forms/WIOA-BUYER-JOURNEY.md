## THE REALITY

A WIOA manager doesn't wake up and decide to buy training. They follow a process. It's bureaucratic, committee-driven, and slow. But it's predictable. If you know the steps, you can stay ahead of them at every stage.

---

## THE JOURNEY — 10 STEPS

### STEP 1: FIRST CONTACT (Day 0)
**What happens:** They receive your email, meet you at a conference, or a colleague forwards your sizzle reel.

**What they do:**
- Glance at the email (60 seconds max)
- If the subject line doesn't grab them, it's deleted
- If it grabs them, they click the sizzle reel link
- They watch 30-60 seconds (not all 72 seconds)
- If Brian seems credible, they bookmark it or forward to a colleague

**What they're thinking:** "Is this relevant to anything I'm working on right now?"

**Your job:** Make the first email about THEIR problem, not your product. Lead with "$850 billion lost" or "your experienced workers are your biggest untapped asset" — not "I have a curriculum."

### STEP 2: THE GOOGLE CHECK (Day 0-3)
**What happens:** If the sizzle reel was interesting, they Google you before responding.

**What they search:**
- "Brian McKinney Learn More Technologies"
- "50+TechBridge"
- "learnmoretechnologies.com"

**What they find (current state):**

**What they're looking for:**

- Does anyone else vouch for him? (testimonials — you don't have these yet)

**Your job:** Make sure everything they find is consistent, professional, and says the same thing. Right now it does.

---

### STEP 3: THE REPLY (Day 3-14)
**What happens:** If they're interested, they reply. But it won't be "yes let's do this." It will be one of:

- "Thanks for reaching out. Can you send more information?"
- "This is interesting. Let me share with my team."
- "We might have something coming up. What's your availability?"
- "Can you send a one-pager or capability statement?"

**What they're NOT doing:** Making a buying decision. They're gathering information to bring to their team.

**Your job:** Reply within 24 hours. Send exactly what they asked for — no more, no less. If they ask for a one-pager, send one page. If they ask for more info, send the AI Business Statement or a 1-page workshop overview. Always include the Calendly link: "Happy to jump on a 15-minute call whenever works."

---

### STEP 4: THE INTERNAL FORWARD (Day 7-21)
**What happens:** The WIOA manager forwards your materials to:
- Their program director
- Their training coordinator
- Their fiscal/budget person
- Possibly their board chair

**What they forward:**
- Your email
- The sizzle reel link
- Whatever one-pager or document you sent
- Their own note: "Saw this, might be relevant to our 50+ population. Thoughts?"

**What the internal team does:**
- Training coordinator checks if the curriculum aligns with their Target Occupations List
- Fiscal person checks if there's budget available
- Program director checks if this fills a gap in their current training offerings

**Your job:** You can't control this step. But you can make it easy by providing materials that answer their questions before they ask:
- What populations does this serve? (Adults 50+)
- What occupations does it train for? (Digital skills, AI foundations — map to SOC codes)
- What does it cost? (Per-user pricing with minimums)
- Is the provider ETPL-listed? (Not yet — but subcontractor path available)
- What are the outcomes? (3X completion, 74% confidence, 347 completions)

---

### STEP 5: THE EXPLORATORY CALL (Day 14-30)
**What happens:** If the internal team sees potential, the WIOA manager schedules a call. This is NOT a sales call. This is a "tell me more" call.

**Who's on the call:**
- WIOA manager (decision influencer)
- Training coordinator (technical evaluator)
- Sometimes the fiscal person
- Sometimes a board member

**What they ask:**
- "Tell us about your program"
- "What populations have you served?"
- "What are your completion rates?"
- "How long is the program?"
- "Can you customize for our population?"
- "Are you on the ETPL?"
- "What does it cost?"
- "Can you provide references?"

**What they're evaluating:**
- Does this person know our world? (Use WIOA language — "eligible populations," "ITAs," "training providers," "Target Occupations List")
- Is the curriculum WIOA-eligible?
- Can we justify this spend to our board?
- Is this a vendor we can work with?

**Your job:** Listen more than you talk. Ask THEM questions:
- "What's your biggest challenge with your 50+ population right now?"
- "What training are you currently offering and where are the gaps?"
- "How do you typically procure training services — ITA or direct contract?"
- "What does your timeline look like?"

End with: "Based on what you've shared, here's what I'd recommend. Let me put together a proposal."

---

### STEP 6: THE PROPOSAL (Day 21-45)
**What happens:** You send a written proposal tailored to what they told you on the call.

**What the proposal includes:**
- Program overview (50+TechBridge — what it is, who it serves)
- Specific recommendation for their organization (based on the call)
- Curriculum outline (6 modules, mapped to their needs)
- Delivery format (Digital, Hybrid, or Enterprise)
- Timeline (30-day deployment available)
- Pricing (per user with minimum, or flat rate for a cohort)
- Outcomes data (347 completions, 3X rate, 74% confidence)
- About Brian / About LMT (credentials, MBE, AARP background)
- References (if available)
- Next steps

**What they do with the proposal:**
- Share with the internal team
- Compare to other options (there may be none — you might be the only one offering this)
- Check budget alignment
- Run it past legal/procurement

**Your job:** Send the proposal within 48 hours of the call. Follow up at 7 days if no response. Follow up again at 14 days. After 21 days with no response, send a "just checking in" with new value (a new article, a new video, a new data point).

---

### STEP 7: THE BUDGET CONVERSATION (Day 30-60)
**What happens:** If the proposal fits, they need to find the money. This is where deals stall.

**Where WIOA money comes from:**
- Individual Training Accounts (ITAs) — requires ETPL listing
- Direct contracts — board discretion, no ETPL needed
- Support services funding — for wraparound services
- Rapid Response funds — for layoff-affected workers
- Governor's Reserve — discretionary state funds

**What delays this step:**
- Budget cycle timing (fiscal year starts July 1 for most boards)
- Competing priorities
- Procurement rules (some boards require 3 bids)
- ETPL listing requirement (for ITA funding)

**Your job:** Ask early: "How does your board typically fund training like this — ITA or direct contract?" If it's ITA, you need ETPL. If it's direct contract, you can start immediately. Help them find the funding pathway.

---

### STEP 8: THE PILOT PITCH (Day 45-75)
**What happens:** Most WIOA managers won't commit to a full deployment first. They want a pilot — a small test to prove it works with their population.

**What a pilot looks like:**
- 15-25 participants
- 1 cohort
- 6-8 weeks
- Digital or Hybrid delivery
- Clear success metrics (completion rate, confidence survey, employment outcomes)

**What it costs them:**
- Digital: 20 users x $249 = $4,980
- Hybrid: 15 users x $750 = $11,250
- Or a flat pilot fee: $5,000-$10,000

**Your job:** ALWAYS offer the pilot. Don't push for full deployment upfront. The pilot is easier to approve, easier to fund, and easier to say yes to. A successful pilot sells the full deployment for you.

Say: "Let's start with a 20-person pilot. If the completion rates match what we've seen — 3X the industry average — we talk about scaling."

---

### STEP 9: THE CONTRACT (Day 60-90)
**What happens:** If the pilot is approved, procurement processes the contract.

**What you need:**
- W-9
- SAM.gov registration (you have this)
- MBE certification (you have this)
- Certificate of insurance (general liability — get this if you don't have it)
- Signed contract or purchase order
- Sometimes: background check, security clearance for facility access

**What takes time:**
- Legal review of the contract
- Board approval (some boards meet monthly)
- Procurement processing (PO generation)

**Your job:** Have all your paperwork ready BEFORE they ask. When they say "we need your W-9 and insurance cert," you send it the same day. Speed = credibility.

---

### STEP 10: DELIVER + EXPAND (Day 90+)
**What happens:** You deliver the pilot. Then you use the results to sell the expansion.

**What you deliver:**
- 50+TechBridge curriculum to the pilot cohort
- Weekly check-ins with the WIOA manager
- Mid-point progress report
- Final report with outcomes data

**What the report includes:**
- Enrollment numbers
- Completion rate (target: 3X industry)
- Confidence survey results
- Participant testimonials
- Recommendations for scaling

**The expansion conversation:**
"Your pilot had a 92% completion rate — 3X the national average. 78% of participants reported feeling more confident with technology. Here's what a full deployment looks like for 100 participants."

**Your job:** Over-deliver on the pilot. The pilot IS the sales pitch for the full contract. Every data point, every testimonial, every success story from the pilot becomes the business case for a $50,000-$200,000 annual contract.
