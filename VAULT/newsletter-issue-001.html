This Week

The Headline

"AI Agents Are Here — and Most People Don't Know What to Do With Them"

The biggest shift in AI right now isn't a new chatbot. It's the move to AI agents — systems that don't just answer questions but take actions on your behalf: scheduling, researching, writing, filing, following up. Every major platform (Google, Microsoft, Apple, Amazon) has released or announced agent capabilities in the past 90 days.

Here's what that means for you: the adults who learn to direct AI agents — not just use them — will have an operating advantage that compounds over time. You don't need to code. You need to know how to give clear instructions and verify results. That is a skill adults 50+ have spent decades developing. It's called management.

Use This

The Tool

Claude.ai

Claude is an AI assistant built by Anthropic — and it is the one I trust most for writing, thinking through decisions, and drafting professional communications. Unlike tools that guess what you want, Claude reads long documents, remembers context across a conversation, and will tell you when it's uncertain rather than making something up.

Try this today: Paste your last LinkedIn post (or an email you wrote) into Claude and say: "Rewrite this to sound more direct and confident. Keep my voice. Remove filler words." See what comes back.
