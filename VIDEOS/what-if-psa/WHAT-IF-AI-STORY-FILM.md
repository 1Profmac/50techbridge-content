# "WHAT IF" — AI Story Film Production Plan
## Learn More Technologies | 50+TechBridge
## Style: AI Narrative Storytelling (Maid & Billionaire method)
## Created: 2026-07-29

---

## THE CONCEPT

Tell the What If story as an **AI narrative mini-film** — not a PSA, not a commercial.
A story the viewer follows emotionally before they know it's LMT.

**The story:** A 58-year-old man gets replaced by AI. Hits rock bottom. Finds LMT. Comes back stronger.

Same message as the PSA. Delivered like a movie.

---

## WHO IS MARCUS?

Marcus is a **fictional composite character** — he represents every real LMT Pioneer.

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| **Full name** | Marcus Johnson (fictional) |
| **Age** | 58 years old |
| **Race** | Black man |
| **Look** | Salt-and-pepper short hair, strong dignified face, dark navy suit |
| **Career** | 28 years at the same company — knows every system, process, and client |
| **What happened** | Flagged by AI workforce optimization. Position eliminated. |
| **His journey** | 47 job applications. 3 interviews. No callbacks. Finds LMT. Comes back. |
| **Why him** | He represents the 347 real Pioneers who walked into LMT thinking technology passed them by |
| **He is NOT** | A victim. He is resilient, dignified, experienced — the most battle-tested person in the room |

Marcus is the emotional bridge between the viewer and LMT.
When the audience sees Marcus succeed — they believe they can too.

## EPISODE STRUCTURE (3-Part Series)

### EPISODE 1 — "THE REPLACEMENT" (3–4 min)
*The problem. The machine world. The fall.*

### EPISODE 2 — "THE ROOM" (3–4 min)
*The turn. The community center. The first smile.*

### EPISODE 3 — "NOT DONE YET" (3–4 min)
*The comeback. The proof. The call.*

Each episode ends on a cliffhanger or emotional hook that pulls to the next.

## EPISODE 1 — "THE REPLACEMENT"

### FULL SCENE-BY-SCENE WITH AI PROMPTS

---

### OPENING TITLE CARD
**Tool:** Canva
**Design:** Black background. White data-display font. Text appears one line at a time.
```
WORKFORCE OPTIMIZATION SYSTEM
PROCESSING...
FLAGGED: WORKERS AGE 50+
RECOMMENDED ACTION: REPLACEMENT
```
**Duration:** 5 seconds
**Sound:** Low hum. Data processing tone.

### SCENE 1 — THE MORNING WALK
**Duration:** 12 seconds
**Voiceover (ElevenLabs — Brian voice):**
*"Marcus had worked at the same company for 28 years."*
*"He knew every system. Every process. Every client."*
*"He thought that meant something."*

**Leonardo AI Prompt:**
```
Black man, 58 years old, dignified bearing, salt-and-pepper hair, dark navy suit,

a man who has walked this path thousands of times. Briefcase in hand. Slight motion
blur on background, sharp on subject. Cinematic, Ridley Scott quality, photorealistic.
Cold blue morning tone. 16:9, 1920x1080.
```

**Kling Animation:** Slow push forward, slight pan left. 12 seconds.
