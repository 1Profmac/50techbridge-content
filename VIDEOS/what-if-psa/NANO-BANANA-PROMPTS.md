## EPISODE 1 — "THE REPLACEMENT"

---

### SCENE 1 — THE MORNING WALK
**Save as:** `ep1-scene1-morning-walk.png`

**Nano Banana Prompt:**
```
Keep Marcus's face and appearance exactly the same.
Show him walking to work on a city street at dawn.
Wide shot, low angle looking up at him slightly.
He's wearing his dark navy suit, carrying a briefcase.
The city is behind him, cold blue early morning light.
His stride is confident — a man who has done this thousands of times.
Cinematic film quality. Cold blue tones.
```

---

### SCENE 2 — THE OFFICE
**Save as:** `ep1-scene2-office.png`

**Nano Banana Prompt:**
```
Keep Marcus's face and appearance exactly the same.
Show him seated at a corporate desk working on his computer.
He is focused and competent.
Around him are younger colleagues in their 20s and 30s — 
moving, talking, collaborating — none of them are looking at him.
He is present but invisible to the room.
Cold fluorescent office lighting. Wide shot showing the contrast.
Slightly washed out colors, cold and clinical.
```

---

### SCENE 3 — THE SCREEN
**Save as:** `ep1-scene3-screen.png`

**Nano Banana Prompt:**
```
Close-up of a computer monitor screen.
Show an HR system interface with Marcus's profile photo on screen.
Text on screen reads: "WORKFORCE OPTIMIZATION IN PROGRESS"
and "RECOMMENDED ACTION: POSITION ELIMINATION"
A progress bar is nearly full.
Cold blue screen glow. Dark room behind the monitor.
Clinical, impersonal, ominous. No human warmth anywhere in the image.
```

---

### SCENE 4 — THE INTERVIEW
**Save as:** `ep1-scene4-interview.png`

**Nano Banana Prompt:**
```
Keep Marcus's face and appearance exactly the same.
Show him sitting across a table from a young white male interviewer in his early 30s.
Marcus is in his navy suit, sitting straight, ready and dignified.
The interviewer is looking at his resume with a closed, distant expression —
he has already made up his mind before asking a question.
Marcus doesn't know yet. He's still expecting engagement.
Cold blue-white office lighting. Tense, quiet injustice.
Cinematic, realistic.
```

---

### SCENE 5 — THE LOWEST POINT
**Save as:** `ep1-scene5-alone.png`

**Nano Banana Prompt:**
```
Keep Marcus's face and appearance exactly the same.
Show him sitting alone at his kitchen table late at night.
Laptop open in front of him showing a job search website.
A cold cup of coffee beside him.
His hands are on the table but he's not typing — just still.
Not crying. Something harder than tears. The weight of a man 
who built something that was taken from him.
One warm lamp. Everything else is dark.
Close-up, intimate. This is his lowest point.
```
