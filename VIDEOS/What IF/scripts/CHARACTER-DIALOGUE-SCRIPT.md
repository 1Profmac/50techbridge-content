## HOW THESE LINES ARE DELIVERED

**Off-camera lines** — speaker is NOT shown talking. Camera stays on the other character's face.
No lip sync required. Record in ElevenLabs using a different voice per character.

**On-camera lines** — speaker IS shown talking. Requires lip sync.
Use HeyGen Photo Avatar: upload character reference image + ElevenLabs audio → talking video.

## CHARACTER 1 — MARCUS JOHNSON
**Age:** 63 | Black man | Protagonist across all 3 episodes
**Voice tone:** Quiet authority. Dignified. Never broken — tired, not defeated.
**ElevenLabs:** Generate a separate voice — deep, warm, measured. NOT Brian's voice.

---

### EPISODE 1 — Scene 6 (Three Workers)
**Delivery:** Off-camera. Camera stays on Tom's face watching the presentation.
**Context:** Standing at the back of the room, Marcus leans slightly toward James.

> *"That's our department."*

Three words. James doesn't respond. He already knew.

---

### EPISODE 3 — The Comeback Interview
**Delivery:** ON-CAMERA. Lip sync required. Use HeyGen Photo Avatar.
**Context:** The interviewer has leaned forward and asked a real question. Marcus answers.

> *"I've seen that system fail three times.*
> *I know exactly where it breaks — and why.*
> *Want me to show you?"*

He doesn't wait for an answer. He already knows it's yes.

**Timing:** Hold on Marcus's face for 2 seconds after last word. Let the interviewer's expression respond.

## CHARACTER 2 — CAROL SMITH
**Age:** 54 | White woman | Machine world — Algorithm Interview
**Voice tone:** Composed. Professional. The voice of someone who prepared and knows it.
**ElevenLabs:** Clear, measured, confident — the way you speak when you've done this before.

---

### EPISODE 1 — Scene 3 (Algorithm Interview)
**Delivery:** ON-CAMERA. Carol speaks directly to the interviewer who is looking at the screen.
**Context:** She has been sitting straight, portfolio open. The interviewer hasn't looked up.
She speaks first — because she's prepared and this is what professionals do.

> *"I've built three teams from the ground up.*
> *I know what you're looking for."*

The interviewer glances up briefly. Then back to the screen.
Camera holds on Carol's face as she realizes the conversation isn't going to start.

**What her face does:** Nothing changes. She has been in this room before.

## CHARACTER 3 — DOROTHY WILLIAMS
**Age:** 64 | Black woman | Human world — The Real Smile (clip-08)
**Voice tone:** Warm. Surprised. Real. Not performing — reacting.
**ElevenLabs:** Natural, slightly breathless — the voice of someone who just figured something out.

---

### EPISODE 2 — The Real Smile
**Delivery:** Off-camera (she's speaking to no one — just herself).
**Context:** She's typed her question. The AI responded. She reads it. Then:

> *"Oh—"*

*[Two seconds of silence. She reads it again.]*

> *"—it actually understood that."*

She types again. Faster. The smile comes after the second response, not the first.

**Note:** Do NOT have her explain what she understood. The audience doesn't need to know.
The face tells the story. The line just makes it real.

## CHARACTER 4 — JAMES OKAFOR
**Age:** 58 | Black man | Machine world — Three Workers (clip-06)
**Voice tone:** Low. Flat. The voice of a man who has stopped being surprised by bad news.
**ElevenLabs:** Quiet, controlled — barely above a murmur. He is not making a scene.

---

### EPISODE 1 — Scene 6 (Three Workers)
**Delivery:** Off-camera. Camera on the optimization screen — the headcount number ticking down.
**Context:** Marcus has just said "That's our department." James responds without looking at him.

> *"I know."*

That's his entire role in this film. Two words that carry 25 years.

## CHARACTER 5 — ROSA GUTIERREZ
**Age:** 52 | Latina woman | Machine world — Kiosk Fail (clip-03)
**Voice tone:** Polite. Trying not to be a burden. The specific tone of someone asking for help
they know they shouldn't have to ask for.
**ElevenLabs:** Clear but soft. Not angry — just tired of this particular obstacle.

---

### EPISODE 1 — Scene 4 (Kiosk Fail)
**Delivery:** Off-camera. Camera on the kiosk screen blinking, indifferent.
**Context:** Rosa has touched the screen three times. Nothing worked. She turns toward the counter.

> *"Excuse me — could someone help me with this?"*

*[No response. The pharmacist is with another customer.]*

Rosa turns back to the screen. Tries one more time. Then sets her items down quietly and walks out.

**What her walking out says:** She is not making a scene. She never makes a scene.
That is what makes it devastating.

## CHARACTER 6 — TOM BRIGGS
**Age:** 61 | White man | Machine world — Authority Screen / Boardroom (clip-04)
**Voice tone:** Neutral. Efficient. He is not the villain. He is the system doing its job
and not noticing the difference.
**ElevenLabs:** Corporate presentation voice. Confident, fast, practiced.

---

### EPISODE 1 — Scene 5 (Boardroom)
**Delivery:** OFF-CAMERA. Camera on the "TARGET DEMOGRAPHIC: 25–40" slide filling the screen.
**Context:** Tom is presenting the product roadmap to the executive team.

> *"The product roadmap targets 25 to 40.*
> *That's our primary growth segment.*
> *We're optimizing acquisition accordingly."*

Nobody in the room questions it.
Camera slowly pans across the executives — all nodding, some typing.

**Note:** Tom is not wrong by his metrics. That is the entire point.
He has never been asked to consider whether the metrics are wrong.

## CHARACTER 7 — DR. LINDA CHEN
**Age:** 59 | Asian woman | Human world — Pioneer Presenting (clip-09/10)
**Voice tone:** Calm expertise. The voice of someone who knows this material cold
and no longer needs to prove it — she just presents it.
**ElevenLabs:** Clear, direct, unhurried. The confidence of someone who did the work.

---

### EPISODE 3 — Pioneer Presenting
**Delivery:** ON-CAMERA. Linda stands at the front of a conference room.
Colleagues — some younger than her — are leaning forward to listen.
**Context:** She is presenting an AI-generated analysis she built herself.

> *"I ran this through the AI tool last night.*
> *Here's what I found."*

*[She clicks to the next slide. The room leans in.]*

> *"The pattern goes back four years.*
> *Nobody caught it because nobody looked here."*

She doesn't say "I'm proud of this." She doesn't need to.
The room's attention says it for her.

## WHAT THIS ADDS TO THE FILM

Without dialogue: Brian narrates a story ABOUT people you watch silently.
With this dialogue: You hear 8 different human voices in one film.

The machine world sounds cold, procedural, corporate.
The human world sounds warm, real, alive.

The contrast between Tom saying "optimizing acquisition accordingly"
and Dorothy saying "oh — it actually understood that"
is the entire film in two lines.
