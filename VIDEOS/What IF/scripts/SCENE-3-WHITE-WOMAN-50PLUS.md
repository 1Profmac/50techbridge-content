## SCENE 3 — THE HIRING ALGORITHM (Revised)
**Timing:** 0:35 – 0:58 (approx. 14 seconds)
**World:** Machine World — cold, clinical, procedural injustice

### Scene Description

An interview room — 2027 or 2028. Slightly ahead.

A wall-mounted screen runs a live AI candidate assessment in real time.

The interviewer — male, 29, well-dressed — watches the screen. Not the candidate.

On screen:
```
CANDIDATE ASSESSMENT
EXPERIENCE: 28 YEARS ✓
SKILLS MATCH: 91% ✓
AGE: 54
FLAG: DEMOGRAPHIC RISK — PROJECTED TENURE < 8 YEARS
RECOMMENDATION: DEPRIORITIZE
```

Carol Smith sits across the table.

She cannot see the screen behind her interviewer.

She has her portfolio open. She prepared three questions to ask at the end.

The interviewer glances at her once.

Then back to the screen.

He has already made a decision that he was told he didn't make.

**VOICEOVER:**
*What if you weren't judged by the color of your hair — or the wrinkles on your skin.
But by the depth of what you know. What you've built. What you've survived.*
