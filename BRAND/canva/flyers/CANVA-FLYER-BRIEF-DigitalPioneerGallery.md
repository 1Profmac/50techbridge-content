- **Canvas size:** US Letter — 8.5 × 11 in (Print)

## BRAND COLORS

| Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy (background) | `#0E1C2F` | Background, header, footer |
| Gold (accent) | `#C8942E` | Headlines, borders, icons, stats |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Body text on dark backgrounds |
| Light gray | `#CCCCCC` | Subtext on dark backgrounds |
| Off-white | `#F8F6F2` | Card backgrounds on white sections |

## FONTS

| Use | Font | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Main headline | Georgia or Canva Serif | Bold + Italic for "Gallery" word |
| Labels / small caps | Montserrat or Arial | All caps, wide letter spacing |
| Body text | Montserrat Light or Arial | Regular weight |
| Stats / numbers | Montserrat | Bold, large size |
