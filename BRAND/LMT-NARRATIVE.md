## THE SHORT VERSION

Nobody was coming to help. So we built it ourselves.

## THE REAL STORY

I spent years inside the system — AARP, workforce programs, community development. I saw how it works. Meetings about meetings. Applications that take 18 months to process. Programs designed in boardrooms that never reach the people they're built for. Billions of dollars allocated for adults 50+ and most of it stuck in bureaucratic traffic jams while the people it's supposed to serve fall further behind every day.

I waited for someone to fix it. Nobody came.

So I built it myself.

Learn More Technologies started with one question: **What if we stopped waiting for permission and just started teaching?**

Not a pilot. Not a study. Not a committee. A curriculum. A classroom. A human being standing in front of other human beings saying: "I know this feels overwhelming. I'm 50+ too. Let me show you."

---

## THE ENGINE

The fuel behind LMT is not funding. It's frustration.

Frustration that 22 million adults 50+ are digitally underserved and the systems built to help them move at the speed of paperwork. Frustration that AI is transforming every industry and the most experienced workers in America are being left behind — not because they can't learn, but because nobody is teaching them. Frustration that the people with 30 years of knowledge, wisdom, and professional instincts are being told they're too old to matter.

**If they won't help us, we'll do it ourselves.**

That's not a tagline. That's the operating principle of this company.

---

## THE VISION — TOO BIG FOR ONE PERSON

50+TechBridge is not a course. It's a movement.

50,000 Pioneers. Not customers — Pioneers. People who learn, then teach. Each one teach one. That's the model. That's how it scales. Not through venture capital. Not through institutional approval. Through people helping people.

One person learns AI. They show their neighbor. Their coworker. Their church group. Their VFW post. Their senior center. The curriculum is free at the foundation. The knowledge spreads like it's supposed to — person to person, community to community.

This is too big for one company. It's too big for one grant. It's too big for one city. That's the point. 50+TechBridge is built to be replicated, not controlled. Plug-and-play. Any community. Any partner. Any facilitator who cares enough to show up.

**We don't scale through sales. We scale through service.**

## THE DIFFERENCE

Funders are looking for profits. Investors want returns. Boards want metrics that justify their budgets.

We're looking for something else.

**Connections.** A 62-year-old woman who was afraid to open a laptop three months ago is now running her small business with AI tools. A 58-year-old veteran who thought the workforce had passed him by just built his first digital portfolio. A 71-year-old grandmother who FaceTimed her grandkids for the first time and cried.

**Tangible results.** Not impressions. Not followers. Not downloads. People whose lives changed because someone sat next to them and said: "You can do this. Let me show you how."

3X completion rate is a stat. But behind that stat is a room full of adults who were told — by the market, by their employers, by the culture — that they were done. And they proved everyone wrong.

## WHO WE'RE FIGHTING FOR

Babies are just little people. Mothers and fathers are the ones who provide, who hold the family together, who care for the children. But grandparents — grandparents are the ones who built the structure. Who support the parents when the bottom falls out or the ground gets soft. They built it. They paid for it. And they are still contributing to it.

And the world is telling them they're done.

That's the lie LMT exists to destroy. These are not obsolete people. These are the people who hold the structure together. When a family hits a crisis, it's not the algorithm that shows up — it's grandma. It's grandpa. It's the person with 30 years of experience who knows how to keep things standing when everything else is falling.

Teaching them AI isn't charity. It's respect. It's recognizing that the people who built this world deserve the tools to keep contributing to it.

## THE DEFINITION OF INFLUENCE

I think influence is misunderstood.

Most people measure it by the amount of money they control or the number of people they connect with. The bigger the network, the more powerful you are. The more capital you deploy, the more influence you have.

I disagree.

**Influence is the number of people you help.**

Not connect with. Not sell to. Not pitch. Help. Actually, tangibly, measurably help. Did their life get better because you showed up? Did they learn something they couldn't learn before? Did they gain confidence they didn't have yesterday?

That's influence. Everything else is just networking.
