## One Brand. One Ladder. Every Offering Has a Name and a Next Step.
### April 13, 2026

---

## THE PROBLEM WITH THE CURRENT SETUP

You have 16+ offerings spread across 4 pages with overlapping names, inconsistent pricing, and no clear path from one to the next. A workforce board director visits /speak and sees keynote topics. An HR leader visits /workforce and sees deployment tiers. An entrepreneur visits /train and sees beginner mobile lessons. None of them see the full picture. None of them know the next step.

The free course teaches "The Portal in Your Pocket" (mobile basics). The workshop teaches "Build an Agentic Business." Those are two different brands serving two different people. Right now they live under one roof and confuse both audiences.

**The fix:** One ladder. Three rungs. Clear names. Every rung leads to the next.

## TIER 1: LEARN (Free — Individual)

**Who:** Any adult 50+ who wants to start

**Tagline:** *"Start here. No cost. No pressure. You're not done yet."*

### Offerings:

| Offering | Format | Price | Current Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **50+TechBridge Foundations** (rename from "Free Course") | 3 self-paced online modules | FREE | Live — /train |
| **#AGENTIC50 Podcast** | Weekly audio | FREE | Live — Spotify, Apple, Amazon |
| **$850 Billion Series** | YouTube video series (Parts 1-4+) | FREE | Live — YouTube |
| **The 50+ Playbook** | Blog / articles | FREE | Live — /the-playbook |

### What changes:
- **Rename the free course** from generic "Free Course" to **"50+TechBridge Foundations"** — signals it's the foundation, not the whole thing. Creates room for what comes above it.
- **Reframe the 3 modules as skill foundations**, not endpoints. Module names stay, but the completion message changes: *"You finished Foundations. Ready to build something? Here's your next step."*
- **CTA at end of free course** → points to Tier 2 (workshop or consulting call), not just "join the community"

### The role of Tier 1:
Tier 1 is **marketing**, not revenue. It builds trust, captures leads, and creates the audience for everything above it. Stop trying to monetize it ($98 paid tier). Make it free forever. The money is in Tiers 2 and 3.

## TIER 2: BUILD (Paid — Individuals + Small Groups)

**Who:** Adults 50+ ready to act — entrepreneurs, career changers, small org leaders

**Tagline:** *"Your experience is the advantage. AI is the accelerator."*

### Offerings:

| Offering | New Name | Format | Price | Audience |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Featured workshop | **"The $76 Trillion Opportunity"** | 90 min from stage | $5,000 | Conferences, events |
| Half-day workshop | **"The Builder's Workshop"** | 3 hours, hands-on | $3,500 standalone / $1,500 add-on to keynote | Corporate, workforce events |
| Lunch-and-learn | **"The 60-Minute Business Case"** | 60 min + Q&A | $1,500 | HR teams, boards, Rotary, faith orgs |
| Keynote | **"I Fight For Us"** (keep) | 45-60 min from stage | $5,000 | Conferences, summits |
| Consulting | **"The Strategy Session"** | 90-min 1:1 or small group | $2,500/session | Founders, org leaders |
| Consulting retainer | **"The Advisory"** | Monthly 1:1 | $3,000–$5,000/mo | Ongoing executive support |

### Why the name changes matter:

**OLD:** "The Agentic Business: How Adults 50+ Are Building AI-Powered Companies"
**Problem:** Decision-makers don't know what "agentic" means. You have to explain the concept before you can sell the workshop.

**NEW:** "The $76 Trillion Opportunity"
**Why:** The number does the work. Event planners hear "$76 trillion" and they don't need an explanation — they need a calendar slot. "Agentic" lives inside the workshop as Brian's framework, not in the title where it confuses buyers.

| Old Title | New Title | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "The Agentic Business" | **"The $76 Trillion Opportunity"** | Sells on impact, not jargon |
| "Half-Day Workshop" | **"The Builder's Workshop"** | Signals hands-on, not passive |
| "Lunch-and-Learn" | **"The 60-Minute Business Case"** | Tells the buyer exactly what their team gets |
| "Workshop Add-On" | Kill this language entirely | Never position your product as an "add-on" — it's a standalone that can be bundled |
| "Train-the-Trainer" | Moves to Tier 3 — **"Certified Facilitator Program"** | It's an org deployment tool, not a workshop |

### The role of Tier 2:
Tier 2 is **revenue + pipeline**. Every workshop generates:
- $1,500–$5,000 in speaking/workshop fees
- 30-50 warm leads (blueprint submissions)
- 1-2 org decision-makers who see the Tier 3 pitch live
- Content for YouTube, LinkedIn, podcast (clips from stage)

### Custom Course Development — Where It Lives

**Current gap:** Organizations ask "can you build something custom for our workforce?" There's no clear offering for this.

**New offering in Tier 2:**

| Offering | Name | Format | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Custom curriculum design | **"The Blueprint"** | Brian designs a custom 4-8 week curriculum for your org's specific workforce challenge | $7,500–$15,000 |

**What "The Blueprint" includes:**
- Discovery call (60 min) — what's the org's challenge, who's the audience, what's the goal
- Custom curriculum outline (4-8 modules, tailored to their industry/workforce)
- Facilitator guide (so their internal team can deliver it)
- Digital assets (slide decks, handouts, worksheets)
- One train-the-trainer session (virtual, 2 hours)
- 90-day support (email access for questions)

**Why this matters for MRR:** "The Blueprint" is a one-time fee, but it creates dependency. The org now has LMT-designed curriculum. When they need updates, new modules, or expanded deployment — they come back. And it feeds directly into Tier 3 licensing.

## TIER 3: DEPLOY (Organizational — B2B + Government)

**Who:** Organizations that want to run the program at scale

**Tagline:** *"Your workforce. Our curriculum. Deployed in 30 days."*

### Offerings:

| Offering | New Name | Format | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Digital deployment | **"50+TechBridge Digital"** | Self-paced, online-only, org-branded | $249/user (min 20 users = $4,980) |
| Hybrid deployment | **"50+TechBridge Hybrid"** | Online + cohort facilitation | $750/user (min 15 users = $11,250) |
| Full deployment | **"50+TechBridge Enterprise"** | Full facilitation + certification + custom | $1,500/user (min 10 users = $15,000) |
| Train-the-trainer | **"Certified Facilitator Program"** | 2-day certification for internal trainers | $2,500/trainer (min 3 = $7,500) |
| Government contracts | **"50+TechBridge GOV"** | WIOA-funded, grant-aligned, custom scope | $50,000–$2,000,000 |

### What changes:
- **Add minimums.** Don't sell single-user licenses. Minimums create real contracts: $4,980 / $11,250 / $15,000 floor.
- **Rename "Full Deployment" to "Enterprise."** Decision-makers at large orgs respond to "Enterprise" — it signals they're getting the top tier.
- **Move Train-the-Trainer here.** It's not a workshop — it's an organizational capability. The buyer is an HR director, not a conference attendee.
- **"50+TechBridge GOV" becomes its own line.** Government procurement needs its own language, its own page, its own proposal templates. GRANT Agent owns this.

### The role of Tier 3:
Tier 3 is **MRR and scale**. Annual licenses, multi-year government contracts, and certified facilitator renewals create recurring revenue. One Enterprise deployment at 50 users = $75,000. One GOV contract = $50K–$2M.

## WHAT TO KILL

| Kill This | Why |
|---|---|
| **$98 paid course tier** | Creates friction at the bottom of the funnel. The free course is marketing, not product. Make it free forever. Revenue comes from Tiers 2 and 3. |
| **"Workshop add-on" language** | Never position your product as secondary. It's "The Builder's Workshop" — standalone. It can be bundled, but it's never an "add-on." |
| **"Agentic Business" in titles** | Keep the concept. Kill the jargon in buyer-facing materials. Use "$76 Trillion Opportunity" externally, teach "agentic" once they're in the room. |
| **Virtual keynote as separate line item** | Just offer it as a delivery option, not a different product. "Keynotes are available in-person or virtual." |
| **Per-user pricing without minimums** | $249/user sounds cheap until someone buys 1 license. Set minimums. Smallest deal = $4,980. |
