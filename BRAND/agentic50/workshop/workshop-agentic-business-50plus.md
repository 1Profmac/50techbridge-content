### PART 2: THE FRAMEWORK (30 min) — Brian teaches

**The 5-Step Agentic Business Blueprint:**

| Step | What | Example from LMT |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **Name your expertise** | What do you know that took 20+ years to learn? | Workforce development, adult education, community building |
| 2. **Find the bottleneck** | What repetitive task eats your time? | Social media posting, email follow-up, grant research |
| 3. **Deploy an agent** | Use AI to handle the bottleneck | SAGE handles 20 posts/week across 6 platforms |
| 4. **Keep the judgment** | You make the decisions. The agent executes. | Brian approves every proposal. GRANT finds them. |
| 5. **Scale without headcount** | Grow revenue without hiring a team of 10 | LMT runs 8 agent roles with 1 human CEO |
