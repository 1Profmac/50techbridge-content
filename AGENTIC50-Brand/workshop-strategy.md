## THE CORE IDEA

Every keynote plants a seed. A workshop makes it stick. The strategy: **never leave the stage without offering the next step.** The workshop is that step.

---

## WORKSHOP FORMATS — 4 TIERS

### 1. LUNCH-AND-LEARN (60 minutes)
**Price:** $1,500
**Audience:** 10–50 people
**Best for:** HR teams, workforce boards, community orgs, Rotary clubs, faith communities
**What they get:**
- 45-minute interactive session + 15 min Q&A
- One focused topic (chosen from menu below)
- 5-page digital takeaway guide (PDF, branded)
- Post-session email with resources + CTA to /workforce

**Why it works:** Low commitment, easy to approve. Gets Brian in the room. Decision-makers see the impact firsthand. Converts to half-day or deployment.

---

### 2. HALF-DAY WORKSHOP (3 hours)
**Price:** $3,000 standalone / $1,500 add-on to keynote
**Audience:** 20–75 people
**Best for:** Conferences, corporate training days, workforce development events
**What they get:**
- Pre-event discovery call (30 min) to customize content
- 3-hour facilitated session with exercises
- Participant workbook (digital, branded)
- 30-day post-workshop email drip (3 emails) with resources
- Certificate of completion for attendees

**Why it works:** Deep enough to create real skill change. The add-on price ($1,500) makes it an easy upsell at keynote booking.

---

### 3. FULL-DAY INTENSIVE (6 hours)
**Price:** $5,000 standalone / $3,000 add-on to keynote
**Audience:** 15–50 people
**Best for:** Enterprise HR teams, government workforce programs, WIOA-funded orgs
**What they get:**
- Pre-event discovery call + audience survey
- 6-hour facilitated session (2 sessions x 3 hours with break)
- Morning: Awareness + data (why this matters)
- Afternoon: Hands-on skills + action planning (what to do Monday)
- Participant workbook + digital toolkit
- 60-day post-workshop support (email access for questions)
- Certificate of completion
- Executive summary report (what was covered, participation, recommendations)

**Why it works:** Justifies WIOA funding. Gives the org a deliverable they can show their board. The executive summary creates the business case for deployment.

---

### 4. TRAIN-THE-TRAINER CERTIFICATION (2 days)
**Price:** $2,500 per trainer (min 3 trainers = $7,500 minimum)
**Audience:** Internal trainers, facilitators, HR staff
**Best for:** Large orgs that want to run the program internally
**What they get:**
- Day 1: Full-day intensive (same as Tier 3) — they experience it as a participant
- Day 2: Facilitation training — how to deliver the curriculum
- Certified Trainer badge + certificate
- Access to full 6-module 50+TechBridge curriculum
- Co-branded materials (org logo + LMT)
- Quarterly check-in call (first year)
- Listed on 50+TechBridge certified trainer directory

**Why it works:** Recurring revenue. Every trainer becomes an ambassador. Org pays once, runs it forever — but stays connected through quarterly calls and curriculum updates.

## BOOKING PROCESS

1. Prospect visits /speak or scans QR
2. Books 20-min discovery call via Calendly
3. Brian does the call — asks: How many people? What's the challenge? What's the budget?
4. Brian sends proposal (keynote, keynote + workshop, or deployment)
5. Contract + invoice via email
6. Pre-event call 2 weeks before
7. Deliver
8. Post-event: send executive summary + upsell to next tier
