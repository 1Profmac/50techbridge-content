# Workshop Video Opening — "The $76 Trillion Opportunity"
## First 3 minutes from stage | Professor Brian McKinney
### Use as: Workshop intro, YouTube video, Short (cut to 60s), LinkedIn video

## SCRIPT (3 minutes)

**[0:00–0:10 — THE HOOK]**

It used to take a team of five to run an online business.

A social media manager. A bookkeeper. A customer service rep. A content writer. An assistant.

Five people. Sixty thousand dollars a year. Before you made a dollar.

**[0:10–0:25 — THE SHIFT]**

Today I run Learn More Technologies with eight AI agents and one human CEO. Me.

A COO agent manages my operations. A social media agent posts across six platforms. A government contracting agent finds opportunities worth millions.

I did not raise venture capital. I did not hire a team of engineers. I started with a folding table and a public library.

**[0:25–0:45 — THE DATA]**

And the data says I am not the exception. I am the rule.

A 50-year-old founder is 2.8 times more likely to build a top-performing company than a 25-year-old. That is MIT — not motivation.

52 percent of all U.S. businesses are owned by people 55 and older.

Adults 50+ hold 76 trillion dollars in wealth.

And McKinsey found that AI can automate 60 to 70 percent of the repetitive work that used to require a full team.

**[0:45–1:15 — THE COST COMPARISON]**

Let me show you what changed.

In 2020, running an online business cost 7,000 to 13,000 dollars a month in contractors and employees.

Social media management alone: 1,500 to 3,000 a month.
Bookkeeping: 300 to 500.
Customer service: 1,500 to 2,500.
Content writing: 400 to 2,000.

Today? The entire AI tool stack costs 300 to 625 dollars a month.

That is a 95 percent reduction.

The barrier that kept experienced professionals from building businesses no longer exists.

**[1:15–1:45 — THE EDGE]**

But here is what the tech industry gets wrong.

They think AI replaces experience. It does not.

AI cannot replace your judgment. It cannot replace 30 years of knowing which client is bluffing. It cannot replace the instinct that tells you when a deal feels wrong. It cannot replace the relationships you built over decades.

AI handles the repetitive work. You handle the decisions that matter.

Your decades of experience give you an edge — AI simply amplifies your strengths.

**[1:45–2:15 — THE 10 THINGS]**

Here are the ten things you no longer need a team for:

Email. Social media. Bookkeeping. Customer support. Content. Your website. Marketing. Scheduling. Reports. Contracts.

Every single one used to require a person on payroll.

Now every single one is a subscription under 50 dollars a month.

**[2:15–2:45 — THE INVITATION]**

So if you have been waiting — for the right moment, the right tools, the right permission — I need you to hear this.

347 Pioneers have already crossed. We are building toward 50,000.

The tools exist. The data is clear. The cost is gone.

The only question left is: what are you going to build?

**[2:45–3:00 — THE CLOSE]**

My name is Professor Brian McKinney. I am the CEO of Learn More Technologies and 50+TechBridge. I am an MBE-certified founder in Austin, Texas. I built my company on AI. And for the next 90 minutes, I am going to show you how to build yours.

You are not done yet.

Let us begin.
