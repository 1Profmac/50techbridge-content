## SCRIPT (read exactly as written)

A 50-year-old founder is 2.8 times more likely to build a top-performing company than a 25-year-old. That's not motivation. That's MIT.

52 percent of all U.S. businesses are owned by people 55 and older. The average age of a top startup founder is 45. 94 percent of adults 50-plus who started businesses chose to.

The data doesn't say you're too late. The data says you're right on time.

And now AI changes everything.

Running an online business used to cost 7,000 to 13,000 dollars a month in people alone. Email. Social media. Bookkeeping. Customer service. Content. A team of five — before you earned a dollar.

Today? One person with AI can handle all of it for under 300 dollars a month.

That's a 96 percent reduction.

It was never about your ability. It was about the barrier to entry. Now, that barrier is gone.

Your decades of experience give you an edge. AI simply amplifies your strengths.

347 Pioneers have already started. We're building toward 50,000. learnmoretechnologies.com. You're not done yet.
