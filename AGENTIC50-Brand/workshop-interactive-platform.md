# Workshop Interactive Platform — Live Resources + Audience Engagement
## "The Agentic Business" Featured Workshop
### Created: April 13, 2026

---

## THE CONCEPT

Before Brian takes the stage, participants have already started. They access a branded online resource page with prep materials, data, and context. During the workshop, they answer live polls, submit questions, and fill out their Agentic Business Blueprint — all from their phone or laptop. After the workshop, the same page becomes their takeaway.

**One URL. Three phases: Before → During → After.**

## PHASE 1: BEFORE THE WORKSHOP (sent 48 hours before)

### What participants receive:

**Email subject:** "Prep for The Agentic Business Workshop — 3 Things to Do Before We Start"

**Email body:**
> You're registered for "The Agentic Business: How Adults 50+ Are Building AI-Powered Companies" with Professor Brian McKinney.
>
> Before we start, visit your workshop page: **learnmoretechnologies.com/workshop**
>
> Three things to do before the session:
> 1. **Watch the 2-minute sizzle reel** — so you know who Brian is and what we're building
> 2. **Read "The 5 Numbers That Change Everything"** — a one-page data sheet that frames the entire workshop
> 3. **Answer the 3-question pre-survey** — Brian will use your answers to customize the session
>
> See you on stage.

### Pre-survey (3 questions, Google Form embedded on page):

1. **What industry did you spend most of your career in?** (dropdown)
2. **Have you ever started a business?** (Yes / No / Thinking about it)
3. **What's the one task in your work or life that eats the most time?** (open text)

Brian reviews answers before the event. Uses them from stage: *"42% of you said email and follow-up eats your time. That's the first agent we're going to build today."*

**Live Poll 3 (Minute 50) — "Your First Agent"**
> "Which agent would you build first?"

*Brian uses this to guide the hands-on exercise.*

**Live Q&A Queue**
Participants submit questions anytime via Slido. Brian addresses the top-voted ones in Part 4 (the close). This means the audience curates its own Q&A — the best questions rise to the top.
