## HOW TO CREATE THE PRINT PNGs

1. Open `BusinessCard-SOURCE.html` in **Chrome**
2. The page shows both cards — "Card Front" and "Card Back"
3. Use **Snagit** to capture JUST the front card (no label, no background)
4. Save as `BusinessCard-FRONT.png` in this folder
5. Capture JUST the back card
6. Save as `BusinessCard-BACK.png` in this folder
7. Do the same for `SpotUV-MASK-FRONT.html` → save as `SpotUV-MASK-FRONT.png`
8. Do the same for `SpotUV-MASK-BACK.html` → save as `SpotUV-MASK-BACK.png`
