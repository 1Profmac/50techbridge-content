## THE 6-SECTION CHAPTER TEMPLATE

Every chapter expands from ~1,800 words to ~6,000 words using this structure:

| Section | What It Is | Words | Pages | You Provide | Claude Writes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. THE HOOK | Bold claim or stat | 500-750 | 2-3 | The insight | The framing |
| 2. THE STORY | Personal narrative | 1,000-1,500 | 4-6 | The actual story | The narrative arc |
| 3. THE PRINCIPLE | The lesson | 1,000-1,500 | 4-6 | The framework | The explanation |
| 4. THE CONTRAST | Old way vs. your way | 750-1,000 | 3-4 | Mistakes you've seen | The argument |
| 5. THE APPLICATION | What to do tomorrow | 750-1,000 | 3-4 | What you tell clients | The steps |
| 6. THE BRIDGE | Tease next chapter | 250-500 | 1-2 | How it connects | The transition |

## THE CHAPTER WRITING PROCESS

### Step 1: Voice Memo (You — 15-30 min)
Open your phone. Talk through:
- The story behind this chapter (who, what, when, where)
- The lesson you learned
- The mistakes you've seen others make
- What you tell people to do about it

### Step 2: Chapter Brief (You — paste into Claude)
```
I'm writing Chapter [X] of "The $76 Trillion Opportunity."

The lesson: [one sentence]

Here's my story: [paste voice memo transcript or bullet points]

Key points I want to hit:
- [point 1]
- [point 2]
- [point 3]

Expand the existing draft at book/chapter-XX-title.md using the
6-section template. Keep my voice — direct, data-driven, warm.
"Adults 50+" never "seniors." Mark [NEEDS YOUR INPUT] anywhere
you're guessing.
```

### Step 3: Claude Drafts (Claude — one session)
Expands to ~6,000 words using the 6-section template.

### Step 4: You Review (You — 30-60 min)
- Does this sound like me?
- Is the story right?
- Is the lesson right?

### Step 5: Claude Revises (Claude — one session)
1-2 revision rounds.

### Step 6: Content Extraction (Claude — one session)
Claude breaks the finished chapter into all platform content automatically.
