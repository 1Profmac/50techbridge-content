## DRAFT ANSWERS

### Q: How is your unique solution meeting the unique needs of your community?

The big institutions talk about digital inclusion. We deliver it — boots on the ground, online and offline, in the communities where adults 50+ actually live and work.

Adults 50+ are the largest and most underserved population in digital skills training. 22 million lack broadband access. One-third of U.S. workers are digitally underserved, with adults 50+ facing the widest gap. Every workforce program in Texas teaches basic computer skills or resume help. None teach AI. The large workforce systems are stuck in bureaucratic traffic jams — applications, certifications, committee reviews — while the population they're mandated to serve falls further behind.

LMT is the David in this space. We're small, fast, and built to move. The only MBE-certified AI training provider in Texas built exclusively for adults 50+. Not adapted. Not modified. Built from day one for this population, by someone who is part of this population.

We don't wait for the system to catch up. We show up — at senior centers, community orgs, libraries, faith communities, employer sites, and online. Plug-and-play: curriculum, facilitation, materials, assessments, and reporting — all included. Community partners provide the participants. We provide everything else. Deployable in 30 days. No bureaucratic overhead. No 18-month build cycle. Just training that works, delivered where people are.

### Q: Impact Aligned — How does your project align with Texas Mutual's workforce development goals?

Texas Mutual invests in postsecondary credentials for middle-skill jobs and supporting small businesses. LMT trains adults 50+ in AI skills that directly apply to both:

- **Middle-skill jobs:** Our curriculum teaches AI tools used in administrative, customer service, project management, and professional communication roles — the exact occupations where adults 50+ are concentrated and most at risk of displacement.
- **Small business support:** Our advanced modules teach AI-powered business planning, marketing, and operations — enabling experienced professionals to launch and grow businesses using AI as a force multiplier.

MBE certification provides procurement alignment with Texas Mutual's commitment to inclusive economic development.

### Q: Intentional Design — How is your program designed to serve the target population?

Every element of 50+TechBridge was designed for adults 50+ from the ground up:

- **Peer instruction model:** Adults learn alongside age peers, not mixed with 20-somethings. Removes the intimidation barrier that kills completion in traditional programs.
- **Experience-first approach:** We start with what participants already know from 30+ years of professional life, then show how AI amplifies that knowledge. Not "learn this new thing" — "apply AI to what you already do."
- **Pacing and accessibility:** Lessons are self-paced with live facilitation available. No timed tests. No assumptions about prior tech experience.
- **Free foundational lessons:** Entry-level content is free and always available. Grant funding extends reach through facilitated workshops and community partnerships — not behind a paywall.

### Q: Addressing Community Needs — What community need does your project address?

In Texas:
- 3.8 million adults 50+ are in the workforce
- Adults 50+ face the largest digital skills gap of any age cohort
- AI adoption among adults 50+ has tripled (9% to 30%) in two years — demand is exploding
- No current ETPL provider in Texas offers AI-specific training for this population
- Workforce boards are mandated under WIOA to serve this population but lack providers who specialize in it

LMT fills a gap that no one else in Texas is filling. The community need is documented by GAO, AARP, RAND, and the Bureau of Labor Statistics. The population is ready. The training providers are absent. We are here.

### Q: Lasting Change — How does your project create lasting impact beyond the grant period?

Three mechanisms ensure impact outlasts the funding:

1. **Free ongoing curriculum:** Foundational AI lessons remain free and accessible to all participants after the grant ends. Learning doesn't stop when funding stops.

2. **Skills that compound:** AI fluency is not a one-time skill. Participants apply AI tools to their work and businesses daily — the value grows over time, not diminishes.

3. **Train-the-trainer model:** Grant funding can include certifying community facilitators who continue delivering the program locally after the grant period. LMT builds capacity, not dependency.

We're not asking for money to build something. We're asking for money to deploy something that already works — and that keeps working after the grant ends.
