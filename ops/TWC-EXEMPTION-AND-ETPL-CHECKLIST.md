## PART 1: CHAPTER 132 EXEMPTION (Do This First)

### Which Exemption Category Applies to LMT

LMT qualifies under **Section 132.002(b)** -- the contract-basis, closed-enrollment, government-funded exemption. This is the strongest fit because:

- LMT does NOT charge tuition directly to students
- Training is financed through workforce programs, grants, and organizational contracts (local, state, or federal funds, or by entities other than the student)
- Training is delivered on a contract basis with closed enrollment (not open to the general public walking in off the street)
- Students are referred through specific programs with eligibility criteria

The statutory language: *"Schools offering a course or courses of special study or instruction financed or subsidized by local, state, or federal funds or by any person, firm, association, or agency other than the student involved, on a contract basis and having a closed enrollment, may apply to the commission for exemption."*

**IMPORTANT:** Section 132.002(a)(3) -- the avocational/recreational exemption -- does NOT apply. That exemption explicitly excludes courses "taught for purposes of preparing persons for paid employment." LMT trains people in workforce skills, so this is the wrong category.

**BACKUP OPTION:** If any individual LMT courses are 24 hours or less AND cost under $500, they may independently qualify under the **Section 132.003 Course Exemption**. This could cover short workshops. But the 132.002(b) exemption covers the whole operation.

#### STEP 6: Check the 132.002(b) Exemption Box (Page 7 of form)

On the form, find the checkbox for **132.002(b)** and check it. Then prepare these FOUR required items:

**Item #1 -- Explanation of the nature of the group(s) of students:**
Write a 1-2 paragraph narrative explaining:
- Who your students are (adults 50+, workforce re-entry candidates, underemployed individuals)
- How they are referred/enrolled (through workforce programs, organizational contracts, grant-funded cohorts)
- What the eligibility or enrollment criteria are (age 50+, referred by partnering organization, enrolled in a specific workforce program, etc.)
- That enrollment is CLOSED -- not open to the general public

**Item #2 -- Explanation of how the courses are of special study, developed by contract:**
Write a 1-2 paragraph narrative explaining:
- Courses are specifically designed for this population (adults 50+ learning AI/digital skills for workforce purposes)
- Each cohort is offered under a specific contract or grant agreement
- Enrollment is limited to students who meet the eligibility criteria of that specific contract
- Courses are NOT open to walk-in members of the general public

**Item #3 -- Copy of the contract signed by responsible parties:**
Attach at least ONE signed contract or grant agreement. This must show:
- Names of the principals (LMT and the funding entity)
- Beginning and ending dates of the contract
- Scope of work or deliverables

If you have multiple contracts (workforce board, organizational partners, grant agreements), include all of them. More evidence = stronger application.

**Item #4 -- (This is actually part of Item #3 on the form):**
The contract copies must include beginning/ending dates and scope of work. This is listed as a separate item on the form but is really about the contract details.

### What Happens After You Mail It

| Timeline | What Happens |
|----------|-------------|
| Day 0 | TWC receives your packet |
| Days 1-14 | TWC logs it, assigns a reviewer |
| Days 15-45 | Reviewer evaluates your application |
| Days 45-60 | You receive one of three outcomes |

**Three possible outcomes:**

1. **GRANTED** -- You receive an exemption letter. Save this forever. You will need it for ETPL.
2. **DEFICIENCY NOTICE** -- TWC needs more information. Respond IMMEDIATELY. This resets your clock.
3. **DENIED** -- You may appeal. The denial letter will include appeal instructions.

**Appeal process:** If denied, you can request a formal hearing before TWC. The denial letter will specify the deadline and procedure. Typically you have 15-30 days to file an appeal.

---

### Common Mistakes That Get Applications Denied

1. **Not proving "closed enrollment"** -- If your website says "enroll now" or "open to all," TWC will deny you. Your marketing must match your application. Training must be limited to specific referred/contracted students, not open to the public.

2. **No signed contracts attached** -- A letter of intent or MOU is not enough. TWC wants executed contracts with dates and scope of work. If you only have one contract, that is fine, but you must have at least one.

3. **Saying the cost is "$0" without explaining funding** -- You must show WHERE the money comes from. Name the grant, the workforce program, the contracting organization. "$0 to student" is the answer, but you need to show the funding source.

4. **Vague course objectives** -- "Teaching digital skills" is too vague. Be specific: "Train adults aged 50+ in ChatGPT prompt engineering, Microsoft 365 productivity tools, and AI-assisted job search techniques to increase employability in technology-adjacent roles."

5. **Checking the wrong exemption category** -- Do NOT check 132.002(a)(3) (avocational). Do NOT check 132.002(a)(4) (employer training). Check 132.002(b) ONLY.

6. **Not including the SOS formation documents** -- The form asks for ownership documentation. Include it.

7. **Sending incomplete forms** -- Every field must be filled in. If something does not apply, write "N/A."

## PART 2: ETPL APPLICATION (Do This After Exemption Is Granted)

The Eligible Training Provider List (ETPL) is what allows WIOA-funded students to use workforce dollars at LMT. You CANNOT apply until you have the Chapter 132 exemption letter in hand.

### Step-by-Step: Getting on the ETPL

#### STEP 1: Get Your Exemption Letter (Part 1 above)

You need the TWC exemption letter as proof you meet Chapter 132 requirements. This is a prerequisite.

#### STEP 2: Contact Your Local Workforce Development Board

LMT is in Austin / Travis County, which means your board is:

**Workforce Solutions Capital Area (WSCA)**
- **Address:** 6505 Airport Blvd, Suite 250, Austin, TX 78752
- **Phone:** (512) 597-7100
- **Website:** https://wfscapitalarea.com
- **General Contact:** info@wfscapitalarea.com

Ask specifically for the **ETP Coordinator**. Say: "I need to apply to get my training programs on the Eligible Training Provider List. Who handles ETP applications?"

If WSCA does not handle your area or you also train in surrounding counties, the board may be:

**Workforce Solutions Rural Capital Area** (covers Williamson, Hays, Bastrop, etc.)
- **Website:** https://workforcesolutionsrca.com
- **Phone:** (512) 244-7966

**TWC ETP Help Desk:** etp.helpdesk@twc.texas.gov

#### STEP 3: Prepare Your ETPL Application Package

The Board will provide you with their specific application forms. Generally you need:

1. **Provider Information:**
   - Legal entity name, address, contact info
   - EIN / Tax ID
   - Chapter 132 exemption letter from TWC
   - MBE certification (include this -- it helps)

2. **Campus Information:**
   - Physical location(s) where training is delivered
   - Hours of operation

3. **Program Information (for each training program):**
   - Program name and CIP code (Classification of Instructional Programs)
   - Program description and objectives
   - Total program hours
   - Total program cost
   - Credential or certificate awarded upon completion
   - Target occupation(s) the program prepares students for
   - SOC codes (Standard Occupational Classification) for target occupations

4. **Alignment with Target Occupations List:**
   - Your programs must prepare students for jobs on the statewide or local Target Occupations List
   - Check the list at: https://texaslmi.com/targetoccupations
   - For LMT, relevant SOC codes likely include:
     - 15-1299 (Computer Occupations, All Other)
     - 15-1232 (Computer User Support Specialists)
     - 43-9XXX (Office and Administrative Support)
     - 11-3021 (Computer and Information Systems Managers)

5. **Business Partnership Documentation:**
   - Letters from local employers stating they would hire graduates of your program
   - Evidence of employer engagement in curriculum development

6. **Student Data Report (SDR):**
   - TWC requires performance data for all students in your programs
   - New providers must submit initial SDR data before listing
   - Track: completion rates, employment rates, median earnings

#### STEP 4: Submit to the Board

The Board reviews your application and may request changes. Once approved at the Board level, they forward it to TWC.

#### STEP 5: TWC Reviews and Lists Your Programs

TWC reviews the Board's submission and your SDR data. If approved, your programs appear on the statewide ETPL.

**Timeline:** The full process typically takes 2-6 weeks after you submit a complete application to the Board. Incomplete applications take longer.

### ETPL Performance Standards (2025-2027 Cycle)

Once listed, your programs must meet these annual benchmarks:

| Metric | Minimum Standard |
|--------|-----------------|
| Employment Rate (Q2 after exit) | 70% |
| Employment Rate (Q4 after exit) | 70% |
| Median Earnings (Q2 after exit) | $7,800 |
| Credential Attainment Rate | 50% |
| Program Completion Rate | 60% |

Failure to meet these standards can result in removal from the ETPL.
