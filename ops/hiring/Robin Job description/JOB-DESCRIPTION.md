## ROLE SUMMARY

You are the CEO's operational second brain — the person who turns Brian's vision, conversations, and scattered notes into organized action. You monitor contracts, grants, and deadlines so nothing falls through the cracks. You don't wait to be told what to do — you see what needs to happen and you flag it, file it, or finish it.

## CORE RESPONSIBILITIES

### 1. Calendar & Schedule Management
- Own Brian's Google Calendar — add, move, reschedule, and protect time blocks
- Confirm meetings 24 hours in advance
- Block prep time before high-stakes calls (grants, contracts, speaking)
- Flag conflicts and double-bookings before they happen

*(AGENT NOTE: A Claude agent with Google Calendar MCP can read/create/update events automatically. It can also send confirmation reminders via Gmail MCP. Human still needed for judgment calls — e.g., which meeting wins when two conflict.)*

**Weekly Deliverable:** Monday morning calendar briefing — email summary of the week's meetings, deadlines, and action items due.

---

### 2. Contracts & Grants Pipeline Tracking
- Monitor the "Contracts & Grants Tracker" spreadsheet in Google Drive (LMT — Contracts & Grants folder)
- Update status on every active opportunity weekly
- Research new grant opportunities and add them to the Pipeline — Pending folder
- Track deadlines and send Brian 7-day and 48-hour warnings before any submission deadline
- File all grant documents, applications, and correspondence in the correct subfolder

*(AGENT NOTE: An agent can scrape grant databases like SAM.gov, Grants.gov, and state portals on a schedule, match against LMT's profile, and auto-populate the pipeline. Status updates still need a human to verify with contacts. Deadline alerts are fully automatable via a cron-triggered agent.)*

**Weekly Deliverable:** Friday end-of-week pipeline report — Google Doc or email summarizing:
- Status changes this week
- Upcoming deadlines (next 14 days)
- New opportunities added
- Blockers or items needing Brian's decision

---

### 3. Email Triage & Correspondence
- Monitor brian@learnmo.com daily (via delegated access)
- Flag urgent items — contracts, grant responses, speaking invitations
- Draft replies for Brian's review on routine correspondence
- File important emails into labeled folders (Grants, Contracts, Speaking, Partnerships)
- Maintain a running Correspondence Log in shared Drive

*(AGENT NOTE: A Gmail MCP agent can label, sort, and surface priority threads automatically. Draft generation is a strong Claude use case — feed it the thread + brand voice guide and it produces on-voice replies. Human review still required before sending.)*

**Weekly Deliverable:** Daily end-of-day email summary (3–5 bullet points: what came in, what's urgent, what was handled).

---

### 4. Contact & Relationship Management
- Maintain contacts in Google Contacts (and HubSpot when applicable)
- After every meeting or call, log: who, what was discussed, next steps, follow-up date
- Send follow-up emails within 24 hours of any meeting Brian attends
- Track key relationships: Maya Williams-Britton (City of Austin), workforce board contacts, AARP connections, speaking contacts

*(AGENT NOTE: A CRM agent could auto-log meetings from calendar events, generate follow-up drafts, and set reminders. Without paid HubSpot API, Google Contacts + a shared Google Sheet is the manual equivalent. An agent could maintain this sheet from calendar + email data.)*

**Weekly Deliverable:** Updated contact log with all interactions from the past week.

---

### 5. Document Preparation & Filing
- Prepare grant applications, proposals, and pitch documents from Brian's outlines
- Format documents to submission requirements (page limits, font, attachments)
- Maintain the shared Drive folder structure — everything in its place
- Convert Brian's voice memos, rough notes, or verbal instructions into clean action items or documents

*(AGENT NOTE: Document formatting and template population is highly automatable. An agent can take a rough outline + grant RFP requirements and produce a formatted first draft. Human needed for final review, signatures, and portal uploads.)*

**Per-Project Deliverable:** Completed draft delivered 72 hours before any submission deadline.

---

### 6. Research & Intelligence
- Research organizations before meetings (who they are, what they fund, key contacts)
- Prepare one-page briefing docs before grant calls or partnership meetings
- Monitor relevant grant portals: SAM.gov, Grants.gov, City of Austin, TWC, AARP Foundation
- Track competitor/peer organizations in the 50+ digital skills space

*(AGENT NOTE: This is the highest-value agent opportunity. A web-scraping agent on a weekly cron can monitor grant portals, pull new RFPs matching LMT's keywords, and generate briefing docs from LinkedIn/org websites before meetings. Build this first.)*

**Per-Meeting Deliverable:** One-page briefing delivered 24 hours before any external meeting.

## REFERENCE: WIOA ETPL — HOW TO GET LMT ON THE LIST

*Robin — this is background context. Brian is handling the initial ETPL application. Your job is to support with data, tracking, and follow-up.*

**What it is:** The Eligible Training Provider List lets workforce boards send LMT funded students via Individual Training Accounts (ITAs) — up to $10,000 per student through WIOA funds.

**Local WDB:** Workforce Solutions Capital Area — (512) 597-7100
**TWC ETP Help Desk:** etp.helpdesk@twc.texas.gov | 800-628-5115

**Steps:**
1. Contact local WDB ETP Coordinator — ask for application packet
2. Verify programs align with TWC Target Occupations List
3. Submit provider + program info to local WDB (org info, program descriptions, costs, duration, credentials, job placement data)
4. Document TX Education Code Ch. 132 licensing exemption
5. Submit Student Data Report (SDR) to TWC — student-level performance data
6. Local WDB reviews → forwards to TWC
7. TWC reviews → posts on statewide ETPL (2–6 weeks)
8. Annual SDR reporting every July

**Your role:** Compile student performance data into SDR format. Track application status. Follow up with TWC. Flag delays.
