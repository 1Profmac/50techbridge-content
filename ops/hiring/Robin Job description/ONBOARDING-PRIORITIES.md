## WIOA ELIGIBLE TRAINING PROVIDER LIST (ETPL) — FULL REQUIREMENTS

**What it is:** Once on the ETPL, workforce boards can send LMT funded students via Individual Training Accounts (ITAs) — up to $10,000 per student through WIOA funds.

**Administrator:** Texas Workforce Commission (TWC) + local Workforce Development Board (WDB)
**Local WDB:** Workforce Solutions Capital Area — (512) 597-7100
**TWC ETP Help Desk:** etp.helpdesk@twc.texas.gov | 800-628-5115
**Portal:** twc.texas.gov/agency/workforce-development-boards/eligible-training-providers

### Steps to Get Listed:

| Step | Action | Details |
|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | **Contact local WDB ETP Coordinator** | Call Workforce Solutions Capital Area (512) 597-7100. Ask for ETP Coordinator. Get the application packet. |
| 2 | **Verify program aligns with Target Occupations List** | LMT's digital literacy/workforce re-entry training must map to an occupation on TWC's statewide Target Occupations List. Digital skills for 50+ maps to multiple target occupations. |
| 3 | **Submit provider + program information to local WDB** | Includes: org info, program descriptions, costs, duration, credentials awarded, job placement data. |
| 4 | **Demonstrate licensing or exemption** | Texas Education Code Chapter 132 governs career schools. LMT likely qualifies for exemption as nonprofit/community education model or grant-funded training (not direct tuition-for-credential). Document this. |
| 5 | **Submit Student Data Report (SDR) to TWC** | Student-level performance data: enrollment, completion, credential attainment, job placement. Use LMT's 347+ Pioneers data. Build SDR-ready tracking NOW. |
| 6 | **Local WDB reviews → forwards to TWC** | Board may request revisions. Respond promptly. |
| 7 | **TWC reviews → posts on statewide ETPL** | TWC may request additional revisions. Timeline: 2–6 weeks from complete submission. |
| 8 | **Annual performance reporting** | Submit updated SDR to TWC every July. Continued eligibility reviewed biennially. |

### Requirements Checklist:
- [ ] Programs align with TWC Target Occupations List
- [ ] Student-level performance data compiled (347+ Pioneers, completion rates, outcomes)
- [ ] TX Education Code Ch. 132 licensing or exemption documented
- [ ] Evidence of business/employer partnerships for each program
- [ ] Meet TWC performance standards (WD 05-19, Change 2)
- [ ] Contact local WDB ETP Coordinator — application started

## TEACHING CREDENTIALS — ADULT 50+ WORKFORCE TRAINING

**No specific state license is required** to run a private workforce training program for adults in Texas. But these credentials make LMT fundable and credible:

### Required / Recommended Credentials:

| Credential | Source | Cost | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TX Education Code Ch. 132 exemption** | Texas Workforce Commission | Free (documentation) | Proves LMT is legally authorized to train without a career school license |
| **TCALL Professional Development** | Texas Center for the Advancement of Literacy & Learning (tcall.tamu.edu) | Free | Recognized credential for adult education instructors — funders look for this |
| **TSLAC Digital Literacy Instructor pathway** | Texas State Library & Archives Commission | Free | THE recognized credential for digital literacy instruction in Texas |
| **WIOA ETPL listing** | TWC + local WDB | Free (application) | Once listed, workforce boards can send funded students directly |
