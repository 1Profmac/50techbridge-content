## NONPROFIT FORMATION

### 1. Texas Secretary of State — SOSDirect
- **Phone:** (512) 463-5555 (Corporations Section)
- **Email:** SOSDirect@sos.texas.gov
- **Portal:** https://direct.sos.state.tx.us
- **Account setup:** https://direct.sos.state.tx.us/acct/acct-subscribe.asp
- **Notes:** File Certificate of Formation (Form 202) for nonprofit. $25 fee. Available 24/7. Must create and fund a client account before filing.

### 2. IRS — EIN Application
- **Phone:** (267) 941-1099 (EIN line, not toll-free) | (877) 829-5500 (Tax-Exempt Orgs help)
- **EIN Application:** https://sa.www4.irs.gov/modiein/individual/index.jsp
- **Hours:** Mon–Fri 6am–1am ET, Sat 6am–9pm ET, Sun 6pm–midnight ET
- **Notes:** Free, instant online. Apply same day Certificate of Formation is approved.

### 3. Pay.gov — Form 1023-EZ Submission
- **Phone:** (800) 624-1373 (Pay.gov Help Desk)
- **Email:** pay.gov.support@fiscal.treasury.gov
- **Direct form:** https://www.pay.gov/public/form/start/29976118
- **Info page:** https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/application-for-recognition-of-exemption
- **Notes:** $275 fee. This is where you submit and pay for Form 1023-EZ.

### 4. Texas Comptroller — Form AP-204 Franchise Tax Exemption
- **Phone:** (800) 252-1381 (franchise tax questions)
- **Email:** exempt.orgs@cpa.texas.gov
- **Form download:** https://comptroller.texas.gov/forms/ap-204.pdf
- **Info page:** https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/exempt/
- **Notes:** Free. File after IRS 501(c)(3) approval. Fillable PDF, requires Adobe Reader. Mon–Fri 8am–5pm CT.

### 5. Fractured Atlas — Fiscal Sponsorship
- **Email:** support@fracturedatlas.org
- **Application:** https://www.fracturedatlas.org/fiscal-sponsorship
- **Sign up:** https://identity.fracturedatlas.org/signup
- **Help center:** https://fracturedatlas.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/
- **Notes:** No phone line. 8% of donations + $10–20/mo. Apply by June 19. Bridge for NEA application.
