## STEP-BY-STEP FORMATION

### Phase 1: Setup (May 2026) — 2 weeks

**Week 1:**
- [ ] Choose the legal name: **50+TechBridge Foundation, Inc.** (or similar — check Texas SOS for availability)
- [ ] Recruit 3 board members (Texas requires minimum 3 directors for a nonprofit corporation):
  - **Brian McKinney** — Founder, board chair
  - **Board Member 2** — someone from your network (Dr. Shantana Robinson? A community partner? A workforce board contact?)
  - **Board Member 3** — someone with nonprofit or finance experience
- [ ] Draft Articles of Incorporation (Texas Form 202)
  - Must include IRS-required 501(c)(3) language:
    - Organized exclusively for charitable/educational purposes
    - No part of net earnings benefits any private individual
    - Upon dissolution, assets go to another 501(c)(3)
- [ ] File Articles of Incorporation with Texas Secretary of State
  - Online: sos.state.tx.us
  - Filing fee: $25
  - Processing: 5-7 business days

**Week 2:**
- [ ] Get an EIN (Employer Identification Number) from IRS — free, instant online at irs.gov
- [ ] Draft Bylaws (governance rules — meeting frequency, officer roles, voting procedures)
- [ ] Hold organizational board meeting — adopt bylaws, elect officers, approve formation
- [ ] Open a bank account for the Foundation (separate from LMT LLC account)
- [ ] Register with Texas Comptroller for state tax exemption (Form AP-204)

### Phase 2: IRS Application (June 2026) — 1 month to prepare

- [ ] Prepare IRS Form 1023-EZ or Form 1023
  - **Form 1023-EZ** (simplified): If projected annual gross receipts under $50,000 and total assets under $250,000. Filing fee: $275. Processing: 2-4 weeks.
  - **Form 1023** (full): If above those thresholds. Filing fee: $600. Processing: 3-6 months.
  - **Recommendation:** Start with **1023-EZ** — you qualify now and it's faster. You can always grow past the threshold later.
- [ ] Prepare required attachments:
  - Narrative description of activities (what the Foundation does)
  - Financial projections (3 years)
  - List of board members and officers
  - Conflict of interest policy
  - Articles of Incorporation (already filed)
  - Bylaws (already drafted)
- [ ] Submit Form 1023-EZ online via pay.gov
- [ ] Wait for IRS determination letter (2-4 weeks for 1023-EZ)

### Phase 3: Post-Approval (July-August 2026)

- [ ] Receive IRS determination letter — you are now officially 501(c)(3)
- [ ] Register with Texas Comptroller for state sales tax exemption
- [ ] Set up donation page (website or PayPal Giving Fund)
- [ ] Draft standard contract between Foundation and LMT LLC for service delivery
- [ ] Update capability statement to include both entities
- [ ] Begin applying for grants as 501(c)(3)

## DIY vs ATTORNEY

**DIY is possible** for 1023-EZ. The form is 3 pages. Many solo founders do it. The risk is getting language wrong in Articles of Incorporation and having IRS reject or delay.

**Attorney recommendation:** A nonprofit formation attorney in Austin charges $500-$1,500 for the full package (articles, bylaws, 1023-EZ filing, state registration). Worth it for speed and peace of mind.

**Free/cheap options:**
- LegalZoom nonprofit formation: ~$99 + filing fees
- Texas RioGrande Legal Aid (if income-eligible)
- University of Texas Law School nonprofit clinic (free, but waitlist)
- SCORE Austin mentor (free business advice, can guide you through it)

## CRITICAL RULES

1. **Keep LMT LLC and Foundation separate.** Separate bank accounts, separate books, separate contracts. Commingling funds kills your tax-exempt status.
2. **Brian can be paid by BOTH entities.** Foundation pays Brian a reasonable salary for Foundation work. LMT pays Brian for LMT work. This is legal and normal.
3. **Foundation contracts with LMT for training delivery.** The Foundation gets a grant → contracts with LMT to deliver the training → LMT invoices the Foundation → Foundation pays from grant funds. Standard in the nonprofit world.
4. **No private inurement.** Foundation funds cannot enrich Brian beyond reasonable compensation. The IRS watches for this.
5. **Annual reporting.** Foundation must file IRS Form 990 or 990-EZ annually. Miss this and you lose your status.
