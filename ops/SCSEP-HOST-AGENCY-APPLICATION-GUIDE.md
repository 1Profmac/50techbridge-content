## 1. WHAT IS A SCSEP HOST AGENCY?

A host agency is a **nonprofit (501(c)(3)) or government agency** where SCSEP participants are placed for **paid, part-time training assignments**. The host agency provides the workspace, supervision, and meaningful training. The SCSEP grantee (AARP Foundation) pays the trainee's wages — **the host agency pays nothing**.

### What LMT Would Do as a Host Agency
- Provide a physical workspace where trainees report
- Assign a site supervisor (Brian or a designated staff member)
- Create meaningful training assignments (digital skills, AI tools — this is LMT's sweet spot)
- Submit electronic timesheets every other Friday
- Support trainees in job searching and provide employment leads
- Participate in quarterly evaluations of trainee progress
- Allow trainees time off (~1 day/month) for SCSEP meetings/workshops
- Consider trainees for permanent hire when vacancies exist

### What AARP Foundation Provides (At Zero Cost to LMT)
- Pays trainee wages (Texas minimum wage or higher — currently state/federal minimum)
- Provides workers' compensation insurance for trainees
- Manages participant enrollment, eligibility, and Individual Employment Plans
- Assigns case managers who check in with trainees and host agencies
- Handles all payroll, taxes, and compliance paperwork for trainees

### Trainee Details
- **Hours:** ~20 hours/week (average)
- **Wages:** Paid by AARP Foundation at minimum wage or prevailing rate (NOT by LMT)
- **Duration:** Assignments typically last 6-18 months per trainee
- **Number placed:** Varies — typically 1-3 per host agency, depends on local demand and your capacity
- **Trainee profile:** Adults 55+, unemployed, household income at/below 125% federal poverty level

Once approved, you will sign a **Host Agency Agreement** that covers:
- Your commitment to provide meaningful training
- Timesheet submission schedule (biweekly, electronic)
- Workplace safety and anti-discrimination compliance
- Agreement to release trainees for monthly SCSEP meetings
- Annual recertification requirement (before June 30 each year)

### Timeline Expectation
- **Week 1:** Phone call + portal registration + local coordinator contact
- **Week 2-3:** Site visit from AARP SCSEP coordinator, paperwork, agreement signed
- **Week 3-6:** First trainee placement
- Total: **3-6 weeks from first call to first trainee on-site**

## 8. CRITICAL QUESTION TO RESOLVE FIRST

**Is LMT a 501(c)(3)?** SCSEP host agencies are defined in federal regulation (20 CFR 641) as nonprofit 501(c)(3) organizations or government agencies. If LMT is a for-profit entity (even with MBE certification), Brian must ask on the call whether there is any pathway for for-profit social enterprises. Some grantees interpret "community-based organization" broadly. If 501(c)(3) status is required and LMT doesn't have it, options include:
1. Partnering with a local 501(c)(3) that serves as the official host agency while LMT provides the training
2. Exploring whether LMT's MBE status + mission qualifies under any exception
3. Establishing a nonprofit arm of LMT

This is the first question to resolve on the phone call.
