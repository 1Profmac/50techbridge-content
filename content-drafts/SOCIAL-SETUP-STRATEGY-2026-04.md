| **Friday** | LinkedIn | Text post (story/lesson) | 9 AM CT |
| **Friday** | FB | Landscape video cross-post | 10 AM CT |

### Batch Workflow
1. **Sunday evening (30 min):** Load week's posts into Metricool
2. **Monday morning (10 min):** Verify first post went out, engage with comments
3. **Daily (5 min):** Reply to comments within 60 minutes of notification
4. **Friday (10 min):** Check Metricool analytics, note what hit
